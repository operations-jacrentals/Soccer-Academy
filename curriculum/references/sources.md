## Governing rules — IFAB Laws of the Game

- ✅ **Ball (Law 2):** circumference **68–70 cm (27–28 in)**, weight **410–450 g**,
  pressure **0.6–1.1 atm** at sea level. → [IFAB Law 2](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-ball/)
- ✅ **Free kick (Law 13):** opponents at least **9.15 m (10 yд)** from the ball.
  → [IFAB Law 13](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/free-kicks/)

_(IFAB sets the ball spec for the full-size game; youth ball sizes 3/4/5 are set
by federations — see US Soccer below.)_

## US Soccer — Player Development Initiatives (2017)

Official small-sided game standards. Format & ball size are ✅ canonical; exact
field/goal ranges are ⚠️ (from a search summary of the PDF I could not open).

| Age group | Format | GK | Ball | Field (L×W, yд) ⚠️ | Goal ⚠️ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U6 / U7 / U8 | **4v4** | no | **3** | 25–35 × 15–25 | 4' × 6' |
| U9 / U10 | **7v7** | yes | **4** | 55–65 × 35–45 | 6.5' × 18.5' |
| U11 / U12 | **9v9** | yes | **4** | 70–80 × 45–55 | 7' × 21' |
| U13+ | **11v11** | yes | **5** | (full) | (full) |

→ Source: [US Youth Soccer — Player Development Initiatives 2017 (PDF)](https://www.usyouthsoccer.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/160/2023/09/Player-Development-Initiatives-2017.pdf)

- ⚠️ **Age-group ↔ age:** US Soccer uses **birth-year** groups (group = season-end
  year − birth year). A chronological **8-year-old typically spans U8→U9** — i.e.
  the 4v4/ball-3 (U8) to 7v7/ball-4 (U9) transition. Confirm each child's group by
  birth year.

## US Youth Soccer — U7/U8 characteristics & method

- ✅ Attention **limited to one task at a time**; high need for **adult approval**;
  enjoy working **with a partner** but struggle with larger groups; **one ball per
  pair**; matches are a **means to development, not the aim**.
  → [US Youth Soccer Coaching Manual (PDF)](https://www.usyouthsoccer.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/160/2023/09/Official-US-Youth-Soccer-Coaching-Manual-1.pdf)
- ✅ **Play-Practice-Play (PPP)** session model; **one focus** per practice; U7/U8
  theme **"Me, the ball, and my friend."**
  → [US Soccer U7–U8 Learning Plan (PDF)](https://www.alsoccer.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/282/2024/03/US-Soccer-Player-Development-Framework-U7-U8-Learning-Plan.pdf)

## The FA — England DNA, Foundation Phase

- ✅ **Four-Corner Model** — technical/tactical, physical, social, psychological.
- ✅ Foundation Phase covers **ages 5–11**, chunked **5–8** and **9–12**; emphasizes
  the **"power of play,"** small-sided games (4v4, 2v2, 4v2), and **opposed** ball
  work / "controlled chaos."
  → [The FA — England DNA Foundation Phase](https://www.thefa.com/bootroom/england-dna-foundation-phase)

## LTAD — Long-Term Athlete Development (Balyi & Way)

- ✅ Stages incl. **Active Start (~0–6)**, **FUNdamentals (~6–9 boys / 6–8 girls)**,
  **Learn to Train (~9–12 boys / 8–11 girls)**, Train to Train, Train to Compete,
  Train to Win, Active for Life. Developed by **Istvan Balyi**.
  → peer-reviewed: [Ford et al. (2011), *J. Sports Sciences* — the LTAD model](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02640414.2010.536849)
- Note: age 8 = **FUNdamentals**, on the cusp of **Learn to Train** (the "golden age").
