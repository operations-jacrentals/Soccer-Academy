# Benfica — Seixal / Benfica Campus

The most **prolific + best-documented** producer among our pure-quantity top 3.
Portugal's model of a modern, sports-science-driven, holistic campus.

## Snapshot

- **Facility:** Benfica Campus, Seixal — opened 2006, ~19 hectares, six pitches, residential (~80 athletes), auditoriums, gyms, injury-prevention and testing labs, the "360S" simulator.
- **Scale:** ~200 staff including ~50 coaches; pathway spans **ages 6–23**.
- **Output:** CIES's current **#1 academy worldwide** (by their weighted index) and ~93 graduates active across pro football. ⚠️ *Unverified — secondary source; see [references/sources.md](../references/sources.md).*

## Philosophy & identity

Holistic development of the **whole player**: technical-tactical proficiency,
physical conditioning, psychological resilience, and formal education — blended
with heavy investment in **sports science and technology**. Winning mentality is
cultivated *through* individual development, not at its expense.

## Structure & pathway

- **CFTs (regional training centres)** act as feeders; identified talents get initial development locally, then progress to the Seixal campus **from ~U13 onward**.
- **Merit-based progression** through age groups toward the B team and first team.
- Strong **feeder-to-first-team** and export pipeline (sell-on model funds the academy).

## Methodology (how they train)

- Integrated **four-corner** development (technical/tactical, physical, psychological, education).
- **Data & analysis embedded from youth up** — the famous **Benfica LAB** (performance analysis, sports science, individualized monitoring, opposition/self analysis).
- Individualized physical development, injury-prevention, nutrition, and psychology built into daily training — not bolted on.
- Video/tactical analysis normalized early (auditoriums for film + lectures).

## Talent ID & evaluation

- Wide scouting net via the CFT network + trials; continuous, data-informed evaluation across the four corners; merit-based selection at each stage.

## What Benfica emphasizes at ages ~8–13

- The **CFT / pre-campus years** are about broad exposure, technical foundation, and small-group development close to home; the **U13 step to Seixal** marks the move to full campus resources — so 8–13 is the *technical-foundation + identification* window before intensive campus specialization.

## What we duplicate (adapted to a homeschool micro-academy)

- **Embed simple "sports science" from day one** — even homemade: track growth/PHV, load, wellness, and a termly testing battery (see [index/09](../index/09-metrics-and-statistics.md)). Benfica's edge is *measurement + individualization*; we can do a lite version cheaply.
- **Holistic four-corner sessions** — never just the ball; always a physical, psychological, and character/education thread ("soccer + smarts").
- **Video & reflection** — film touches, review together, ask questions. Benfica normalizes analysis young; a phone + a question does most of it.
- **Individual development plans** — a living per-child plan with targets across the four corners.
