# Ajax — De Toekomst

The original blueprint (Cruyff carried its ideas to Barcelona) and — for *us* —
the most **directly applicable** model, because its individualized, developmental,
**Montessori-inspired** philosophy fits a homeschool academy almost perfectly.

## Snapshot

- Academy at **Sportpark De Toekomst** ("The Future").
- Identity: **attractive, offensive, creative, fast, fair** football, built on a recognizable **4-3-3**.
- Famous for producing an outsized number of pros and for a clearly published methodology (public development manuals exist).

## Philosophy & identity — TIPS

- **TIPS = Technique · Insight · Personality · Speed** — each with **ten criteria**, used to assess and develop every player.
  - **Technique:** ball mastery, both feet.
  - **Insight:** reading the game — spatial awareness, decisions, positioning, anticipation.
  - **Personality:** character, resilience, independence.
  - **Speed:** of action and of thought, not just legs.
- **Play recognizably**: offensive, creative, fast, fair, and *preferably in the opponent's half*.

## Methodology (how they train)

- **Development over winning** — explicitly: *"the philosophy of winning at all cost will not be tolerated; winning is a by-product of what and how coaches teach."* The **individual's development outranks the team result.**
- **Montessori-inspired individualization** — *"every player's development trajectory is unique"*; the club builds an environment where each child progresses **at their own pace**, guided by expert coaches in a carefully prepared setting. (This is homeschooling's core idea, applied to football.)
- **Creativity first at the youngest ages** — coaches aim to develop **"creators and inventors, not tacticians"**; technique through fun, engaging, ball-heavy activities.
- **60+ positional rondo variations**, scaling from **4v1** circles for young players to **8v4** for older groups.
- Sessions maximize **individual ball touches**.

## Age-stage structure (their 5 stages)

| Stage | Age | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4–6 | play, fun, first touches |
| 2 | **7–9** | technique & creativity; creators not tacticians |
| 3 | **10–12** | technique + emerging game insight; positional rondos |
| 4 | **13–15** | tactical awareness, positional play, 4-3-3 roles |
| 5 | 16–18 | performance, position-specific, competition demands |

> **Our 8–13 window spans Ajax stages 2–4** — i.e. *technique & creativity* →
> *technique + insight* → *the start of tactical/positional structure*. That
> progression is a ready-made spine for our age-by-age plan.

## Talent ID & evaluation

- Assessed continuously against the **TIPS** criteria; individual trajectories tracked rather than judged only on results; late developers protected by the development-over-winning ethos.

## What we duplicate (adapted to a homeschool micro-academy)

- **Make development-over-winning the explicit rule** — write it into our charter; results are feedback, not the goal. Easy to honor in a homeschool setting with no league pressure.
- **Lean into the Montessori overlap** — individualized pacing is already how homeschooling works; apply it to football with per-child TIPS-style profiles.
- **Protect creativity in the youngest years (8–9)** — encourage 1v1s, tricks, and improvisation *before* drilling structure; "creators, not tacticians" first.
- **Use TIPS as our evaluation rubric** — Technique / Insight / Personality / Speed is a simple, coach-friendly scorecard that maps cleanly onto our [four-corner tags](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md).
- **Adopt their age-stage spine** for sequencing 8→13.
