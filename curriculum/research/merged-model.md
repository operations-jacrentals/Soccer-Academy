# Merged Model — the Intersection of Benfica, La Masia & Ajax

This sweep merges the three academies **by commonality, not by best-of.** We keep
only what **all three share**; anything unique to a single club is deliberately
**set aside** (listed at the end). The intersection *is* the model we adopt —
proven three times over, and robust precisely because none of it depends on one
club's budget, culture, or star coach.

> Method: for each dimension of an academy, compare all three and extract only the
> **common ground**. If it isn't in all three columns, it doesn't make the model.

## The merged model (the seven shared principles we adopt)

1. **One game model, every age.** A single, coherent, proactive/possession-based
   identity taught the same way from the youngest group up — so everything
   compounds and nothing has to be un-learned.
2. **Technique + intelligence before physicality.** Select for and develop the
   brain and the touch; let athleticism come through games and maturation. "Train
   brains before bodies."
3. **Rondos, small-sided games, maximum touches.** The daily training core — high
   involvement, real decisions, minimal lines/queues.
4. **Development over winning.** The individual's growth outranks the result;
   scoreboard is feedback, not the goal.
5. **The whole player.** Four corners (technical, physical, psychological, social)
   **plus** education and character — never just the ball.
6. **Individualized & long-term.** Each child on their own trajectory with a
   living development plan; patience with late developers.
7. **Continuous, rubric-based evaluation.** Assess the whole player regularly
   against a simple scorecard (we'll use a four-corner / TIPS-style rubric).

---

## Deliberately set aside (unique to one club → excluded by the intersection rule)

Kept out *not* because they're bad — several are excellent — but because they
aren't common to all three, and our rule is to merge on shared ground:

- **Benfica:** the 19-hectare residential campus, dedicated sports-science lab, and analyst department (Benfica LAB). *Resource/scale-specific.* → we take the **principle** (measure & individualize) at homeschool scale, not the infrastructure.
- **La Masia:** strict *tiki-taka* dogma and the extreme "small technician only" selection. *Too identity-specific.* → we keep possession/positional *tendencies*, not the dogma.
- **Ajax:** the single rigid **4-3-3** as the only formation, and the explicit "Montessori" branding. → we keep **individualized development** (which all three share) without mandating one formation or one label.
- **Any single acronym** (TIC vs TIPS): → we adopt the shared four-corner *substance*, using one vocabulary consistently rather than one club's brand.

## The merged model, in one line

> **A single proactive, possession-based game model, built on technique and game
> intelligence, taught through rondos and small-sided games with maximum touches,
> inside a development-over-winning culture that grows the whole child on an
> individualized, long-term plan — reviewed against a simple four-corner rubric.**

## How it plugs into the age 8–13 build

This merged model is the **method layer** for every age. For each year 8 → 13 we
pull the age-appropriate building blocks from the [index](../index/README.md) and
teach them *this* way: one game model, rondos + SSGs + max touches, development
first, whole child, individual plans, termly rubric. See the age-by-age spine in
[`synthesis-and-blueprint.md`](synthesis-and-blueprint.md).
