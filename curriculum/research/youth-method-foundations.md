# Youth Method Foundations — how the best systems develop young players

The **young-age "how"** behind the talent-producing nations (Netherlands, Spain,
Portugal, Brazil/Argentina) and our three academies — anchored to the elite
systems, **not** to convenience sources. Cited per the
[Documentation Standard](../DOC_STANDARD.md).

> **Why this file exists:** an earlier draft leaned on US Soccer's method because
> it's the most citable in English. But the US isn't a top talent producer, so the
> *method and philosophy* are re-anchored here to systems that are. US Soccer /
> IFAB are kept **only** for neutral regulatory numbers (ball/field sizes, laws),
> which converge with FIFA/KNVB anyway.

## 1. Futsal & the small-space foundation ✅

The talent hotbeds grow players on **futsal / small-space** football first:

- Confined space → **far more touches**, tighter control, faster decisions than
  11-a-side. Portugal built futsal into schools and grassroots; Spain transitions
  gradually futsal → 11v11; Brazil is futsal-first. Figo and Rivaldo are cited as
  futsal-raised.
  → [FIFA Training Centre — talent pathways in Brazil & Spain](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/environment/research-brief/high-performance/talent-pathways/talent-pathways-in-brazil-and-spain.php)
  · [Learning from Portugal's futsal success](https://www.cjffutsal.com/the-future-of-us-futsal-learning-from-portugals-success-story)
- **For us:** run a **futsal-style, maximum-touch** small-sided environment.

## 2. Coerver Coaching — the ball-mastery pyramid (Dutch) ✅

Developed by Dutch coach **Wiel Coerver** (1970s–80s) to fix a lack of individual
skill. Its **Pyramid of Player Development** (six blocks) is a ready spine:

1. **Ball Mastery** (foundation — touches & control, both feet)
2. **Receiving & Passing** (first touch, accurate passing)
3. **1v1 Moves** (beating an opponent, creating space)
4. **Speed** (of body *and* mind)
5. **Finishing**
6. **Group Play** (individual skill → team play)

Its **"5 S's"** — Skills, **Smarts**, Speed, Strength, Spirit — even name *Smarts*,
mapping onto our "soccer + smarts" mission.
→ [Coerver Coaching](https://coerver.com.au/%E2%80%9Cwithout-individual-technical-skills-nothing-can-happen%E2%80%9D-coerver-academy-coaching-headmaster)
· [The Coerver method (VOOR Sport)](https://voor.sport/en/mag/the-coerver-method-more-than-just-ball-mastery)

## 3. Dutch (KNVB) — street football + child-centred small-sided ✅

- Rooted in **street football** (kids playing daily, self-organized, all skills
  developed by playing).
- **Child-centred**: optimize enjoyment *and* development.
- Small-sided by age: **2v2 (4–5), 4v4 (U7 minis), 6v6 (U8/U9)**.
  → [KNVB — Dutch Youth Football](https://knvb.h5mag.com/dutch_youth_football/the_decision)

## 4. Spanish/Catalan (La Masia) — rondo + positional ✅

- **Rondo** + small-sided games + the three Ps; technique and decision-making from
  the youngest ages. → [Barça Innovation Hub](https://barcainnovationhub.fcbarcelona.com/blog/the-models-of-youth-teams-in-football/) · see [barcelona-la-masia-model.md](barcelona-la-masia-model.md)

## 5. National systems — native-language primary sources (translated) ✅

Straight from the federations, in their own languages (see
[references](../references/sources.md) for links + the translation caveat):

- **🇪🇸 Spain (RFEF):** 8-year-olds are **Benjamín (8–9)**, playing **Fútbol-7**. The
  sequencing rule is explicit: *"técnica individual primero, conceptos colectivos
  después"* — **individual technique first, collective concepts later** — via the
  **Método Integrado** (ball in every task). This is the sourced backbone of our
  technique-first approach across 8→13.
- **🇳🇱 Netherlands (KNVB):** **U8–U10 play 6v6** (42.5 × 30 m); the ref is a
  **spelbegeleider** ("game leader") who lets kids *discover* the game; **"deliberate
  play"** (street-like, child-centred); **no standings published** at these ages.
- **🇵🇹 Portugal (FPF / Benfica):** published **player-development stages**
  (football + futsal); Benfica's **"metodologia interdisciplinar."**

> **Format reality (sources differ → we note the range):** the elite systems use
> **6v6 (NL) to Fútbol-7 (ES)** at ages 8–9, plus **futsal (5v5)** — all *reduced,
> max-touch* formats. We run a **futsal-style small-sided** environment in that
> band rather than any single number.

## What this means for age 8 (the elite anchor)

- **Environment:** futsal-style small-sided, maximum touches (Futsal / KNVB).
- **Content spine:** the **Coerver pyramid** — ball mastery → receiving/passing →
  1v1 → group play.
- **Ethos:** street-football freedom + **development over winning** (KNVB, Ajax).
- **Regulatory only from US/IFAB:** ball/field/format sizes (converge with FIFA/KNVB).
