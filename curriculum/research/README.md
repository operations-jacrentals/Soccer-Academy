# Academy Model Research

Reference research on the academies we're modeling the Soccer-Academy program on.
These are the "how" — proven development systems — that we pair with our
[knowledge index](../index/README.md) (the "what") to build the curriculum.

## How we got to these three

A deliberate funnel (see chat history / the research reports):

1. **Most professional players produced, by quantity** (worldwide, currently active — CIES data) → River Plate, Benfica, Defensor Sporting led the raw counts. ⚠️ *CIES figures are secondary/unverified (primary fetch blocked) — see [references/sources.md](../references/sources.md).*
2. **Most publicly available data, documents, and studies to actually learn from** → **Benfica** was the clear winner of that group, and the exercise surfaced that the two *most-documented* academies in the world are **Barcelona (La Masia)** and **Ajax (De Toekomst)**.
3. **Final selection:** the three richest, most-replicable, best-documented models —

## How this feeds our curriculum

Our plan: **cover every important building block, age-by-age, from 8 → 13, in order.**

- The **[index](../index/README.md)** is the checklist of building blocks (skills, moves, attributes, tactics, psychology…).
- These **three models** tell us *how to stage and teach* those blocks — and, crucially, **what each emphasizes at each age**. Ages 8–13 sit squarely in the LTAD **"golden age of learning"** (`L2T`, ~9–12) that all three treat as the technical-foundation window.
- The **[synthesis](synthesis-and-blueprint.md)** distills the shared DNA into a concrete blueprint adapted to *our* context: a homeschool, 5-year, "soccer + smarts" micro-academy.

## The shared DNA (preview)

All three, despite different cultures, converge on the same principles:

- A **single, coherent philosophy of play** taught identically at every age.
- **Technique + game intelligence before physicality** — "train brains before bodies."
- **Positional play & rondos**, **small-sided games**, and **maximum ball touches**.
- **Development over winning** — results are a by-product.
- **Holistic development** — technical, tactical, physical, psychological, *plus* education and character.
- **Long-term, individualized** player pathways.
