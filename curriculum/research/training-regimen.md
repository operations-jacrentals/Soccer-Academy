# Training Regimen — what the top 3 clubs actually do

The **session and weekly structures** used by Benfica, La Masia, and Ajax — the
sourced replacement for the (American) *Play-Practice-Play* model. Cited per the
[Documentation Standard](../DOC_STANDARD.md).

---

## The session — ball-first, rondo-led ✅

- **🇪🇸 La Masia (Barcelona):** the ball is in play from minute one. **Rondo is the
  warm-up** — activation *and* an introduction to the day's topic — then a
  **positional game** (*juego de posición*, e.g. 4v4+3 / 5v5+3) to learn the
  solution, then a **training game / match** to apply it; plus finishing circuits,
  GK work, and set pieces. → [A session at FC Barcelona](https://juegodeposicion.wordpress.com/2020/11/24/a-session-in-fc-barcelona/) · [9campnou](https://9campnou.com/all-you-need-to-know-about-fc-barcelona-training-sessions-2021-guide/)
- **🇳🇱 Ajax:** sessions open with **15–20 min of progressive rondo**, then larger
  exercises. A documented **thirds** structure: **⅓ body control (warm-up) · ⅓
  individual play · ⅓ group play (small-sided game)**; **60+ rondo variations**
  (4v1 → 8v4). → [Ajax youth development](https://www.ajaxuniverse.com/youth-development) · [ASC Ajax development manual (PDF)](https://dt5602vnjxv0c.cloudfront.net/portals/7416/docs/asc%20ajax%20players%20development%20manual.pdf)
- **🇵🇹 Benfica:** interdisciplinary, sports-science-led; refines technical execution
  under pressure and decision-making across phases. → [SL Benfica — Futebol Formação](https://www.slbenfica.pt/en-us/futebol-formacao/formacao)

**→ Our session shape (replaces Play-Practice-Play):**

> **Warm-up → Individual play (skill / 1v1) → Group play (small-sided game)**
> — everything with the ball (Ajax's thirds + Barça's rondo-warm-up). ✅
> The **① Warm-up is a rondo by default** (Barça's signature); its form flexes to the
> day (ball-manipulation in the early ball-mastery weeks). The **day's focus lives in
> the ② Individual block** — that's what changes day to day.

## The week — Tactical Periodization / the morphocycle ✅

- **🇵🇹 Tactical Periodization** (Vítor Frade, Portuguese school; ~40 yrs old, forged
  at FC Porto): physical, technical, tactical, and psychological preparation are
  **inseparable dimensions** trained *together* around the game model —
  *"unbreakable wholeness."* The **weekly morphocycle** ("mother cell") varies each
  day's emphasis and intensity — building through the week, then tapering — with
  named days (acquisition / recovery / tension / endurance / activation).
  → [Barça Innovation Hub — Tactical Periodization](https://barcainnovationhub.fcbarcelona.com/blog/tactical-periodization-football-load-planning/) · [What is Tactical Periodization?](https://jobsinfootball.com/blog/what-is-tactical-periodization/) · [Vítor Frade](https://www.tacticalperiodisation.com/pt/vitor-frade/)

**→ Our weekly regimen (adapted).** We run **15 sessions/week (3×/day × 5 days)** with
no fixed match, so we borrow TP's *principles* rather than its match-week calendar:
- **Integrated sessions** — never isolated fitness; the ball and a game-idea are always present.
- **Varied daily emphasis** — rotate the four corners + deliberate play across the week.
- **Build-and-recover rhythm** — alternate higher- and lower-intensity days; protect recovery.

## What we take vs. adapt

| From | Take | Adapt for age 8 |
|---|---|---|
| La Masia | rondo-as-warm-up, ball-first, positional games | keep positional play **simple** ("creators, not tacticians") |
| Ajax | the thirds (warm-up / individual / group) + 60 rondos | more **deliberate play** (futsal/street) per the early-engagement evidence → [mastery model](../mastery-model.md) |
| Benfica / TP | integrated week, build-and-recover rhythm | no fixed match — rotate corners instead of match-day countdown |
