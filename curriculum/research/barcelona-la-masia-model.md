# Barcelona — La Masia

The most **studied** youth academy in the world, and the clearest example of a
single **philosophy of play** taught identically from the youngest ages to the
first team. Backed by the **Barça Innovation Hub**, which openly publishes
research and courses — a gift for anyone designing a curriculum.

## Snapshot

- Founded **1979**; reformulated by **Johan Cruyff** in the late 1980s (inspired by Ajax's model).
- Philosophy: **technical skill over physicality**, ball possession, **positional play**, high pressing, intelligent decision-making, collective movement.
- Backed by the **[Barça Innovation Hub](https://barcainnovationhub.fcbarcelona.com/)** (research, a Diploma in Football Coaching, a Professional Master's in the FC Barcelona methodology).

## Philosophy & identity — TIC & the "three Ps"

- **TIC = Technique · Intelligence · Competitiveness.** The player they build: technically excellent, tactically intelligent, competitive.
- **The three Ps = Possession · Position · Pressing.** The team identity every age group plays.
- A **unified game model**: a La Masia player learns the *same* concepts the first team uses, so promotion is seamless.

## Methodology (how they train)

- **Juego de Posición (positional play)** taught from the earliest ages: the pitch divided into zones/lines/half-spaces; movement off the ball to create or deny space; keeping possession under pressure; unlocking defenses through collective movement, not athleticism.
- **The rondo** as the signature drill — "train brains before bodies": **scan first, receive second, execute under pressure.** A daily staple at every age.
- **Small-sided games** and a high **volume of ball touches** per session.
- **Age- and maturity-tailored** loads, with strong injury-prevention and recovery.
- **Education alongside football** — academic schooling is part of the model.

## Talent ID & evaluation

- Selects for **technique + football intelligence over size/athleticism** (famously develops smaller, brainier players — "playmakers, not athletes"). Rigorous, long-term evaluation; explicit institutional commitment to the long game over short-term youth results.

## What La Masia emphasizes at ages ~8–13

- These are core **positional-play + rondo + technical-mastery** years: build the *decision-making brain* and first touch early, inside the club's one game model. Small-sided formats, constant touches, and "think before you receive" habits are installed now — physicality is deliberately *not* the selector.

## What we duplicate (adapted to a homeschool micro-academy)

- **Adopt ONE game model and teach it at every age** — pick our identity (possession + positional play is a proven, teachable default) so every session reinforces the same picture.
- **Make the rondo our daily bread** — it scales from 3v1 upward, needs almost no space/equipment, and trains scanning, first touch, and decisions at once. Perfect for a small group.
- **Select-for-and-develop the brain** — reward good decisions and scanning over speed/size, especially with mixed-age homeschool groups.
- **Mine the Barça Innovation Hub** — it *publishes* the methodology (positional play, ball exit, pressing, set pieces); use it as our open-source coaching library.
