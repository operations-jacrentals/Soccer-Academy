# Age 8 — Session & Weekly Template

The repeatable shape of every 1-hour session — taken from **our top-3 clubs**
(Ajax's thirds + Barça's rondo-warm-up), organized across the week by **Tactical-
Periodization** principles. No US model.

> **Provenance:** the **session shape** (Warm-up → Individual → Group) and the
> **integrated, varied week** are ✅ **sourced** →
> [references — training regimen](../references/sources.md). The **minute
> allocations, the 3-session split, and example activities** are ✏️ **coaching
> judgment**. Where the day fits in the year: [cycle plan](01-cycle-plan.md).

## The session shape — Warm-up → Individual → Group ✅

Ajax's documented **thirds** (⅓ warm-up · ⅓ individual · ⅓ group), with the warm-up
run as a **rondo** à la La Masia. Everything with the ball, from minute one.

> **The day's focus lives in the Individual block** (②) — that's what changes day to
> day. The **Warm-up** (①) is a **fixed slot whose form flexes**: a **rondo** by
> default (Barça's signature), but in **Week A** (the ball-mastery week) it's a
> ball-manipulation warm-up — a true rondo needs a little passing, so rondos are
> the everyday warm-up in **Weeks B & C**. A rondo trains first touch, scanning,
> passing and decisions at once, so it **primes** the focus rather than competing
> with it.

| Part | What it is | Source | Time ✏️ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **① Warm-up (rondo by default)** | ball-manipulation **or** a simple **rondo** (3v1/4v1) — activate *and* introduce the day's topic | 🇪🇸 La Masia rondo · 🇳🇱 Ajax warm-up third ✅ | ~15 min |
| **② Individual play — the day's focus** | the day's single focus — ball mastery, dribbling, 1v1, passing, technique | 🇳🇱 Ajax thirds · Coerver ✅ | ~15 min |
| **③ Group play** | a **small-sided game** / simple positional game / futsal — apply it; ref as **game-leader** | 🇪🇸 La Masia game · 🇳🇱 KNVB ✅ | ~15 min |
| **④ PR Time** | the day's **three self-referential measures** (each vs the child's own PR to beat) + Courage Counts — **coach-led, protected time to chase the record** | ✏️ our design | ~10 min |
| **Huddle** | one question + the **word of the day**; praise effort | — | ~5 min |

> **PR Time is a dedicated block, not an afterthought** ✏️: always the **last
> active block** (fixed placement keeps fatigue constant, so marks stay
> comparable session to session) → [reports & card](04-reports-and-player-profile.md) ·
> [tracker spec](interactive/DESIGN-SPEC.md).

### The within-session load curve

Intensity is not flat across the session — it follows a curve: the **Warm-up ramps
up**, the **Individual** block is technical/moderate, the **Group small-sided game
is the session's peak** (SSGs reach **~80–90% of max heart rate** ✅ → [references](../references/sources.md)),
and the **Huddle cools down**. That whole curve then **scales with the day's load**
in the weekly rhythm — **Mon ease-in · Tue build · Wed peak · Thu recover · Fri
light** (**tactical periodization** ✅; the exact pattern ✏️ →
[cycle plan](01-cycle-plan.md)) — a *recover* day sits low, the *peak* day runs
hot. So it's the **week's build-and-recover rhythm, one level down.** (The shape
is ✅ sourced; the exact per-phase percentages are ✏️ illustrative.)

## The day: three sessions, one plan ✏️

A day is **three 1-hr sessions** (3 × 1 hr = 3 hrs) — and **all three run the same
plan.** Repeating the day's plan builds **same-day mastery**: players meet a skill
in the morning session and own it by the evening session. The structure and split
are identical in each session (Warm-up → Individual → Group → PR Time); the **focus changes
day to day**, climbing Week A → B → C across the [3-week cycle](01-cycle-plan.md)
and **levelling up loop over loop**.

- **Attendance = 3 ticks per day** (one per session; 1 tick = 1 hour). The day's
  **best mark per measure is the single PR** — recorded across all 3 sessions, it
  **only ever climbs**. A completed day = all 3 sessions. ✏️
- **Physical develops *inside* each session** (warm-up movement + games; **LTAD:** no
  formal fitness at this age) — not a separate session. ✅
- **Deliberate play** lives in the **Group** phase of every session ✅ (KNVB →
  [references](../references/sources.md)).
- **Recovery** sits **between** the three sessions; across the week keep the
  **build-and-recover** rhythm. **No standings** ✅ (KNVB).

## Coaching principles (age 8)

- **Ball-first** — the ball is in play from the first minute (La Masia). ✅
- **Rondo is the engine** — control under pressure, scanning, decisions. → [index/03](../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md)
- **Technique first, collective concepts later** ✅ (RFEF).
- **Let them discover** — coach as *game-leader* (KNVB); say the cue, don't lecture.
- **Development over winning** ✅; effort and trying are the headline.
- **Adjust with STEP** — Space · Task · Equipment · People → [index/10](../index/10-coaching-styles-and-pedagogy.md).

## Example session — Technical (Focus: change of direction) ✏️

1. **① Warm-up (15m):** ball-mastery in a box (sole rolls, both feet) → a **3v1 rondo** if ready.
2. **② Individual (15m):** change-of-direction move (inside/outside/sole) vs. a passive → active "shark." Cues: **"Both feet," "Head up."**
3. **③ Group (15m):** small-sided game; celebrate any change of direction that beats a defender.
4. **④ PR Time (10m):** the day's three measures — gate run, weak-foot gate run, slalom — each vs their own PR to beat; count Courage.
5. **Huddle (5m):** "When did changing direction help you?" Word of the day: **"Head up."**

## Setup & safety

- **Ball**: size 3 (younger) → size 4 — regulatory only ✅ → [references](../references/sources.md).
- **Futsal-style small spaces** for touches; water breaks; injury-prevention; limit deliberate heading → [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md).

> To adapt for another focus, keep the **Warm-up → Individual → Group → PR Time** shape and swap
> the **Individual-block focus** and the word of the day — and keep any drill swap
> **in the day's skill family** ([family lock](04-reports-and-player-profile.md)).
