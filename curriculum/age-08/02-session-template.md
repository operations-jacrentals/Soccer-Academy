# Age 8 — Weekly Session Template

The repeatable shape of every age-8 session, built on **Play-Practice-Play**.

> **Provenance:** **Play-Practice-Play (PPP)**, **one focus per session**, **one
> ball per pair**, and *matches-as-a-means* are ✅ **sourced** (US Soccer / US
> Youth Soccer — [references/sources.md](../references/sources.md)). The **minute
> allocations and the example drills** are ✏️ **coaching judgment** aligned to the
> short-attention-span guidance (attention = one task at a time ✅).

## The shape — Play → Practice → Play ✅

| Phase | What it is | Time ✏️ |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrival** | a ball each the moment they arrive; free dribbling/tag | first 5 min |
| **PLAY (1)** | a small-sided game (up to 4v4) — let them play, watch, minimal talk | ~15 min |
| **PRACTICE** | one focused activity on the day's **single focus**; start simple, add a challenge; **pairs / one ball each** | ~20 min |
| **PLAY (2)** | back to a small-sided game so the focus shows up in a real game | ~15 min |
| **Huddle** | 1–2 questions + the **word of the day**; praise **effort** | ~5 min |

*Total ≈ 60 min* ✏️ (fit it to your schedule; keep blocks short).

## Coaching principles (age 8)

- **One focus** ✅ — everything points at the day's theme.
- **Max touches** — a ball each or one per pair ✅; **no lines/queues**.
- **Say the cue, don't lecture** — use the [words of the year](../index/13-teaching-cues-and-acronyms.md); let them play.
- **Freeze sparingly** — a few seconds to show a picture, then release.
- **Encourage constantly** — U8 players have a **high need for adult approval** ✅; praise trying and effort over outcome.
- **Development over winning** — the scoreboard is not the point.
- **Adjust on the fly with STEP** — Space · Task · Equipment · People → [index/03](../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md).

## Example session (Focus: change of direction) ✏️

1. **Arrival (5m):** ball each, free dribble; on "freeze," stop the ball with the sole.
2. **PLAY 1 (15m):** 4v4 to two small goals; no coaching, just watch who changes direction well.
3. **PRACTICE (20m):** *"Ball each in a box"* — dribble and, on a call/cone, change direction (inside/outside/sole), both feet. Add a "shark" (light defender) once comfortable. Cue: **"Both feet," "Head up."**
4. **PLAY 2 (15m):** 4v4 again; celebrate any change of direction that beats a defender.
5. **Huddle (5m):** "When did changing direction help you?" Word of the day: **"Head up."**

## Safety & setup

- Age-appropriate **ball size 3** (U8) / size 4 (U9) ✅ → [references](../references/sources.md).
- Water breaks; small goals; limit deliberate heading at this age → [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md).

> To adapt for another focus, keep the PPP shape and swap only the **Practice**
> activity and the **word of the day**.
