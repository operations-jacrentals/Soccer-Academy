# Age 8 — Term Plan

How the [age-8 targets](README.md) spread across the year. Read with the
[birds-eye TOC](README.md) and the [Documentation Standard](../DOC_STANDARD.md).

> **Provenance:** the **method** (Play-Practice-Play, one focus per session, one
> ball per pair) and the **U7/U8 theme** ("Me, the ball, and my friend") are ✅
> **sourced** ([references/sources.md](../references/sources.md)). The **term
> split and drill themes** are ✏️ **coaching judgment** aligned to those sourced
> priorities.

## Age-8 planning principles

- **Technique runs all year** — terms shift *emphasis*, they don't gate skills. ✏️
- **One focus per session** ✅ (US Soccer) — pick it and stay on it.
- **Short blocks** — attention is **one task at a time** ✅ (US Youth Soccer).
- **Games, not lines**; **one ball per pair** ✅; development over winning.
- **The three terms mirror the sourced U7/U8 theme** ✅ *"Me, the ball, and my
  friend"* — self → ball → teammate → game.

---

## Term 1 — "Me & the Ball" (ball mastery & fun) ✏️

*Anchor:* the "Me, the ball" start of the US Soccer theme ✅.

- **Lead focus:** ball mastery, dribbling, running with the ball, first touch, **individual creativity** ("creators, not tacticians").
- **Blocks:** [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md) (control, first touch), [index/02](../index/02-skill-moves.md) (body feint, step-over).
- **Physical:** movement ABCs through games → [index/04](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md).
- **Psych/Social:** enjoyment & confidence; routines, listening, managing kit.
- **Smarts:** count touches; left vs. right foot.
- **Words:** "Head up," "Both feet," "Try it!"
- **Before we move on:** every child *enjoys* having a ball, dribbles in space with some head-up, tries a move without fear. ✏️

## Term 2 — "Me, the Ball & a Friend" (1v1 & first combinations) ✏️

*Anchor:* the "…and my friend" middle of the theme ✅; U8 players **enjoy working with a partner** ✅.

- **Lead focus:** beating a defender 1v1 (moves in games), running with the ball at pace, **intro passing & the give-and-go**, intro finishing.
- **Blocks:** [index/02](../index/02-skill-moves.md) (feints), [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md) (passing, finishing), [index/03](../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md) (mini-rondo 3v1, 1v1 games).
- **Physical:** change of pace/direction through games.
- **Psych/Social:** bravery to try 1v1s; sharing, taking turns.
- **Smarts:** simple **dribble-vs-pass** decisions with prompting.
- **Words:** "Find space," "Both feet."
- **Before we move on:** attempts moves *in games*; can play a simple 1-2 with a partner; shares the ball. ✏️

## Term 3 — "Playing the Game" (small-sided games) ✏️

*Anchor:* matches are a **means to development, not the aim** ✅.

- **Lead focus:** 4v4 small-sided games — finding space, basic rules, **intro defending (jockey)**, everyone tries **goalkeeping**.
- **Blocks:** [index/07](../index/07-tactics-and-systems.md) (find space, principles — kept minimal), [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md) (4v4, rules), [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md) (defending, GK basics).
- **Physical:** game-based endurance/agility.
- **Psych/Social:** teamwork, respect for teammates/opponents/rules; "Next play."
- **Smarts:** which goal is ours/theirs; in/out of play; spatial awareness.
- **Words:** "Find space," "Next play."
- **Before we move on / before 9:** plays 4v4 with less clumping; knows core restarts; bounces back from mistakes. ✏️

---

## Notes

- **Not rigid:** a child ahead of the group keeps progressing; a beginner spends
  longer in Term 1. Individualize via each child's **IDP** → [assessment](03-assessment.md).
- **Session structure:** every session uses **Play-Practice-Play** → [session template](02-session-template.md).
- **Cadence** (sessions/week, term length in weeks) is a family/scheduling choice
  ✏️ — set it to fit the homeschool calendar; frequency and total contact time
  matter more than the exact split.
