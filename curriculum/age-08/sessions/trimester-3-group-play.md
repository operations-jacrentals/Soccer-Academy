# Age 8 · Trimester 3 — Group Play (day library)

A **5-day circuit** (D11–D15) for **Trimester 3** of the [trimester plan](../01-trimester-plan.md) —
one week's worth of day-types that **repeats each week** across the ~13-week trimester.
Coerver pyramid block: **Group Play** (individual skill → team play). Theme:
*"Playing the Game."* Environment: **futsal-style small-sided** ✅.

> **Provenance:** Coerver Group Play + small-sided/rondo (**La Masia / futsal**) and
> **let-them-discover** (**KNVB** *spelbegeleider*) are ✅ sourced; specific
> activities/order are ✏️ coaching judgment. Warm-up → Individual → Group, **no
> standings**. Tactics stay minimal — "creators, not tacticians." **The circuit
> repeats weekly** — rotate the drill inside each theme and **progress** across the ~13 weeks.

---

### D11 — Small-sided games & finding space ✏️ (KNVB / futsal ✅) · intensity: ease-in
- **Words:** "Find space."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **positional rondo** — hold your zone; coach as **game-leader**, minimal whistle.
- **Individual (20m):** "find the free player" — 3v1 / 4v2 keep-ball; move to a passing angle.
- **Group (20m):** 4v4 to two goals; reward spreading out over clumping.
- **Huddle:** "Where was the space — near friends or away from them?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] support play · [Ps] scanning · [S] teamwork · **Smarts:** space = away from others.

### D12 — Rondo (keep the ball) ✏️ (La Masia ✅) · intensity: build
- **Words:** "Head up," "Both feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **4v1 rondo.**
- **Individual (20m):** **3v1 rondo** — pass, support angles, one-touch when ready; rotate the middle.
- **Group (20m):** 4v4 with a "keep 4 passes = a point" rule.
- **Huddle:** "How did you keep the ball from the defender?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] passing under light pressure · [Ps] decisions · [S] communication.

### D13 — Intro defending: jockey & poke ✏️ · intensity: **PEAK**
- **Words:** "Stay on your feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** shadow the partner (mirror movement).
- **Individual (20m):** **jockey** (don't dive in) → **poke** the ball; stay goal-side; 1v1 & 2v1.
- **Group (20m):** small-sided game; praise patient defending & winning it back.
- **Huddle:** "Why not dive in straight away?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] defending · [P] balance · [Ps] patience.

### D14 — Goalkeeping taster (everyone) ✏️ · intensity: recover
- **Words:** "Ready position."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **Circle Catch** — roll & catch in pairs; soft "W" hands.
- **Individual (20m):** GK **ready position**, scoop a rolling ball, **roll/throw** it out; each child has a go.
- **Group (20m):** small-sided **with** a keeper — rotate GK often.
- **Huddle:** "What's tricky about being the keeper?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] handling · [Ps] bravery · [S] empathy (everyone tries).

### D15 — Festival, rules & year-end checkpoint ✏️ (laws → [index/12](../../index/12-laws-and-formats.md)) · intensity: light
_Merges rules-through-play and the year-end festival; learn the game by playing it._
- **Words:** "Our goal / their goal," "Next play."
- **Warm-up (15m):** free game; coach pauses to name **in / out of play**.
- **Individual (20m):** learn by doing — **throw-in, goal/corner restart, no hands (except GK)**; "which goal is ours?"
- **Group (20m):** small-sided **festival** refereed as a **game-leader** (explain, don't punish) — just play, celebrate everything, **no standings** ✅.
- **Checkpoint:** complete the [assessment & IDP](../03-assessment.md) — set the handoff to **age 9**; each child names one thing they're proud of.
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; **Smarts:** directions / sequence / which goal is ours.
