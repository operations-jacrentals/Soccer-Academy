# Age 8 · Trimester 3 — Group Play (session library)

Six runnable sessions for **Trimester 3** of the [trimester plan](../01-trimester-plan.md).
Coerver pyramid block: **Group Play** (individual skill → team play). Theme:
*"Playing the Game."* Environment: **futsal-style small-sided** ✅.

> **Provenance:** Coerver Group Play + small-sided/rondo (**La Masia / futsal**) and
> **let-them-discover** (**KNVB** *spelbegeleider*) are ✅ sourced; specific
> activities/order are ✏️ coaching judgment. Rondo → Individual → Group, **no standings**.
> Tactics stay minimal — "creators, not tacticians."

---

### S13 — Small-sided games & finding space ✏️ (KNVB / futsal ✅)
- **Words:** "Find space."
- **Warm-up (5m):** free small-sided; coach as **game-leader**, minimal whistle.
- **Skill (15m):** "find the free player" — 3v1 / 4v2 keep-ball; move to a passing angle.
- **Game (20m):** 4v4 to two goals; reward spreading out over clumping.
- **Huddle:** "Where was the space — near friends or away from them?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] support play · [Ps] scanning · [S] teamwork · **Smarts:** space = away from others.

### S14 — Rondo (keep the ball) ✏️ (La Masia ✅)
- **Words:** "Head up," "Both feet."
- **Warm-up (5m):** pairs passing.
- **Skill (20m):** **3v1 rondo** — pass, support angles, one-touch when ready; rotate the middle.
- **Game (15m):** 4v4 with a "keep 4 passes = a point" rule.
- **Huddle:** "How did you keep the ball from the defender?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] passing under light pressure · [Ps] decisions · [S] communication.

### S15 — Intro defending: jockey & poke ✏️
- **Words:** "Stay on your feet."
- **Warm-up (5m):** shadow the partner (mirror movement).
- **Skill (15m):** **jockey** (don't dive in) → **poke** the ball; stay goal-side; 1v1 & 2v1.
- **Game (20m):** small-sided game; praise patient defending & winning it back.
- **Huddle:** "Why not dive in straight away?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] defending · [P] balance · [Ps] patience.

### S16 — Goalkeeping taster (everyone) ✏️
- **Words:** "Ready position."
- **Warm-up (5m):** roll & catch in pairs.
- **Skill (15m):** GK **ready position**, scoop a rolling ball, **roll/throw** it out; each child has a go.
- **Game (20m):** small-sided **with** a keeper — rotate GK often.
- **Huddle:** "What's tricky about being the keeper?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] handling · [Ps] bravery · [S] empathy (everyone tries).

### S17 — Rules & restarts through play ✏️ (laws → [index/12](../../index/12-laws-and-formats.md))
- **Words:** "Our goal / their goal."
- **Warm-up (5m):** free game; coach pauses to name in/out of play.
- **Skill (15m):** learn by doing — **throw-in, goal/corner restart, no hands (except GK)**; "which goal is ours?"
- **Game (20m):** small-sided game refereed as a **game-leader** (explain, don't punish).
- **Huddle:** "What happens when the ball goes out on the side? the end?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] game understanding · [S] respect for rules · **Smarts:** directions/sequence.

### S18 — Festival & year-end checkpoint ✏️
- **Words:** "Try it!," "Next play."
- **Warm-up:** small-sided **festival** — just play, celebrate everything, **no standings** ✅.
- **Skill:** child's-choice stations (autonomy) revisiting the year.
- **Checkpoint:** complete the [assessment & IDP](../03-assessment.md) — set the handoff to **age 9**.
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; each child names one thing they're proud of.
