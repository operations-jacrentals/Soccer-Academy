# Age 8 · Week C — BENFICA · Playing the Game · day library

**Week 3 of the repeating [3-week cycle](../01-cycle-plan.md)** — five day-plans
(**D11–D15**) that come back around **every loop (~13× a year)**. Academy identity:
**Benfica** — athlete & finish / compete ([research](../../research/benfica-model.md)).
Coerver pyramid blocks: **Finishing + Group Play** (individual skill → team play).
Environment: **futsal-style small-sided** ✅.

> **Provenance:** Coerver Finishing/Group Play + small-sided/rondo (**La Masia /
> futsal**) and **let-them-discover** (**KNVB** *spelbegeleider*) are ✅ sourced
> ([references](../../references/sources.md)); the academy identity is ✅ researched;
> specific activities and the ✏️ re-sequenced day order are coaching judgment on the
> [drill catalog](../../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md).
> Warm-up 15 → Individual 20 → Group 20 → Huddle 5, **run ×3 that day** (3 ticks),
> **no standings**. Tactics stay minimal — "creators, not tacticians." **Rotate
> drills inside each theme and level up loop over loop.** **PR Time** closes each
> session — a single day-PR per measure, only climbs →
> [reports & card](../04-reports-and-player-profile.md).

### D11 — Finishing: placement, both feet ✏️ (Coerver: Finishing ✅) · intensity: ease-in
- **Words:** "Head up, pick your spot," "Next play."
- **Warm-up (15m):** dribble & shoot at mini-goals.
- **Individual (20m):** **side-foot placement** up close → **laces** with a short run-up; both feet; then the pattern **pass → 1‑2 → finish** (unopposed → light defender).
- **Group (20m):** small-sided game — look for a 1‑2 before a shot; celebrate composed finishes over blasts.
- **Huddle:** "Corner or keeper — where did you aim?" "Next play" after a miss.
- **PR Time ✏️:** Finish Ten /10 · Weak-foot Finish /10 · First-time Finish /10 — **placement, never power**.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] finishing · [Ps] composure, "next play" · **Smarts:** which corner (spatial).

### D12 — Speed & agility with the ball ✏️ · intensity: build · *maturation-guarded*
_Playful athleticism, always with a ball — never a fitness test. **LTAD ✅: no formal
fitness testing at 8**; these measures are logged only as a **light monthly personal
best**, off the daily flow ([reports](../04-reports-and-player-profile.md))._
- **Words:** "Quick feet," "Head up."
- **Warm-up (15m):** "traffic" dribbling with speed calls (walk → jog → zoom); freeze-and-turn.
- **Individual (20m):** **dribble-dash races** (short bursts, full rest, ball each); **SAQ-ladder** steps then straight onto a ball; decelerate-and-turn games.
- **Group (20m):** relay races **with the ball** · tag games; everyone runs, nobody's time is read out.
- **Huddle:** "When did quick feet help you in the game?"
- **PR Time ✏️ (monthly only):** 20-yd Dash PB · Dribble-Dash 20yd · SAQ-ladder clean reps — a slower month is **never** read as a weakness (biology's clock ✅).
- **Corners/Smarts:** [P] agility, accel/decel · [T] ball at speed · [Ps] effort over outcome.

### D13 — Defending 1v1: jockey & win ✏️ · intensity: **PEAK**
- **Words:** "Stay on your feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** shadow the partner (mirror movement).
- **Individual (20m):** **jockey** (don't dive in) → **poke** the ball; stay goal-side; 1v1 & 2v1.
- **Group (20m):** small-sided game; praise patient defending & winning it back.
- **Huddle:** "Why not dive in straight away?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Jockey Clock (patience PB, cap 10s — longer = better) · Poke-steal /10 (no foul) · **Patient win-backs → 🦁 lion**.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] defending · [P] balance · [Ps] patience.

### D14 — Goalkeeping taster: "Safe Hands" (everyone) ✏️ · intensity: recover
- **Words:** "Ready position."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **Circle Catch** — roll & catch in pairs; soft "W" hands.
- **Individual (20m):** GK **ready position**, scoop a rolling ball, **roll/throw** it out; each child has a go.
- **Group (20m):** small-sided **with** a keeper — rotate GK often.
- **Huddle:** "What's tricky about being the keeper?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Keeper's Ten /10 (W-catch) · Scoop-and-roll /10 · Reaction saves — brave saves → 🦁 lion.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] handling · [Ps] bravery · [S] empathy (everyone tries).

### D15 — Festival, compete & rules ✏️ (laws → [index/12](../../index/12-laws-and-formats.md)) · intensity: light · *checkpoint*
_Merges rules-through-play and the festival; learn the game by playing it — and
**compete** the healthy way: uncounted vs the opponent, celebrated vs yourself._
- **Words:** "Our goal / their goal," "Next play."
- **Warm-up (15m):** free game; coach pauses to name **in / out of play**.
- **Individual (20m):** learn by doing — **throw-in, goal/corner restart, no hands (except GK)**; "which goal is ours?"
- **Group (20m):** small-sided **festival** refereed as a **game-leader** (explain, don't punish) — just play, celebrate everything, **no standings** ✅.
- **Checkpoint:** quick [assessment](../03-assessment.md) note per child each loop (less clumping? knows restarts? bounces back?); **on the final loop of the year**: complete the IDP and set the handoff to **age 9** — each child names one thing they're proud of.
- **PR Time ✏️:** Rules Five /5 (unprompted) · Fair-play acts · Festival involvement — *celebrated, never ranked*.
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; **Smarts:** directions / sequence / which goal is ours.
