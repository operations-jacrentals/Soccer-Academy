# Age 8 · Week B — LA MASIA · Passing ("Me, the Ball & a Friend") · day library

**Week 2 of the repeating [3-week cycle](../01-cycle-plan.md)** — five day-plans
(**D6–D10**) that come back around **every loop (~13× a year)**. Academy identity:
**La Masia** — position & passing, the rondo as the engine
([research](../../research/barcelona-la-masia-model.md)). Coerver pyramid blocks:
**Receiving & Passing**.

> **Provenance:** same frame as [Week A](week-a-ajax-ball-mastery.md) — the Coerver
> progression & technique-first rule are ✅ sourced ([references](../../references/sources.md));
> the academy identity is ✅ researched; specific activities and the ✏️ re-sequenced
> day order are coaching judgment on the
> [drill catalog](../../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md).
> Warm-up 15 → Individual 20 → Group 20 → Huddle 5 (the warm-up is a **rondo** most
> days this week), **run ×3 that day** (3 ticks); ball each / one per pair, no
> standings. **Rotate drills inside each theme and level up loop over loop**
> (two-touch → one-touch, bigger → smaller grid). **PR Time** closes each session —
> a single day-PR per measure, only climbs; this week's streaks are **cooperative:
> the group vs its own record**, never child vs child →
> [reports & card](../04-reports-and-player-profile.md).

### D6 — Inside-foot passing ✏️ (Coerver: Passing ✅) · intensity: ease-in
- **Words:** "Both feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **3v1 rondo.**
- **Individual (20m):** **inside-foot pass** to a partner/gate; weight & accuracy; **both feet**; two-touch → one-touch; count completions (smarts).
- **Group (20m):** passing gates game — score by passing through cones to a teammate.
- **Huddle:** "Was your pass too soft, too hard, or just right?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Pass Count 60s (pair) · Target Hits /10 · Weak-foot Pass Count 60s.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] passing · [S] cooperation · **Smarts:** count/score passes.

### D7 — Give-and-go (1‑2) ✏️ · intensity: build
- **Words:** "Pass and move."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **4v1 rondo.**
- **Individual (20m):** **wall pass / give-and-go** around a cone "defender"; pass, move, receive.
- **Group (20m):** 3v1 mini-rondo → 2v2 with a target; reward a 1‑2.
- **Huddle:** "What did you do right after you passed?" (move!)
- **PR Time ✏️:** One-Two Count 60s (pair) · Wall-pass streak · Through-gate 1‑2 /10.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] combination · [Ps] anticipation · [S] teamwork.

### D8 — Rondo: keep the ball ✏️ (La Masia ✅) · intensity: **PEAK**
- **Words:** "Head up," "Both feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **4v1 rondo.**
- **Individual (20m):** **3v1 rondo** — pass, support angles, one-touch when ready; rotate the middle; level up loop over loop (smaller grid, touch limits).
- **Group (20m):** 4v4 with a "keep 4 passes = a point" rule.
- **Huddle:** "How did you keep the ball from the defender?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Rondo Streak · Passes-before-loss · Split passes (through the middle) — all **group vs its own record**; one-touch tried → 🦁 lion.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] passing under light pressure · [Ps] decisions · [S] communication.

### D9 — Receiving on the half-turn + scanning ✏️ (Coerver: Receiving ✅) · intensity: recover
- **Words:** "Check your shoulders."
- **Warm-up (15m):** "scan when you can" — receive & turn, open body; spot who has a ball.
- **Individual (20m):** **receive on the half-turn** to play forward; **scan** before receiving (coach flashes a number behind them = smarts).
- **Group (20m):** 4v2 keep-ball — turn and find the free player.
- **Huddle:** "What did you see before the ball came?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Half-turn Count 60s · Receive-turn-pass time · **Scan Score /10 → Smarts**.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] receiving · [Ps] scanning/awareness · **Smarts:** read the number.

### D10 — Positional keep-ball / find space ✏️ (La Masia / KNVB ✅) · intensity: light · *checkpoint*
- **Words:** "Find space."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **positional rondo** — hold your zone; coach as **game-leader**, minimal whistle.
- **Individual (20m):** "find the free player" — 3v1 / 4v2 keep-ball; move to a passing angle; switch the play when a side jams.
- **Group (20m):** 4v4 to two goals; reward spreading out over clumping.
- **Huddle + checkpoint:** quick [assessment](../03-assessment.md) note per child (plays a 1‑2? shares? scans before receiving?).
- **PR Time ✏️:** Team Keep-ball Streak · Support-angle receives · Switch-of-play count — **group vs its own record**.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] support play · [Ps] scanning · [S] teamwork · **Smarts:** space = away from others.
