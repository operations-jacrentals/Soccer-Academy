# Age 8 · Trimester 1 — Ball Mastery (day library)

A **5-day circuit** (D1–D5) for **Trimester 1** of the [trimester plan](../01-trimester-plan.md) —
one week's worth of day-types that **repeats each week** across the ~13-week trimester.
Coerver pyramid block: **Ball Mastery** (+ intro receiving). Theme: *"Me & the Ball."*

> **Provenance:** the **progression** (Coerver Ball Mastery → the pyramid) and the
> **technique-first, max-touch** frame are ✅ **sourced**
> ([foundations](../../research/youth-method-foundations.md) · [references](../../references/sources.md)).
> The **specific activities, counts, and day order** are ✏️ **coaching
> judgment**, drawing on the [drill catalog](../../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md).
> Every day-plan uses the [Warm-up → Individual → Group shape](../02-session-template.md)
> (the **day's focus is the Individual block**) and is **run ×3 that day**; a **ball
> each**, ~60 min ✏️, **no standings** (KNVB ✅).
>
> **The circuit repeats weekly.** A day-type runs ~13 times across the trimester, so
> **rotate the specific drill inside each theme week to week** (e.g. gates one week,
> a Cruyff-turn circuit the next) and **progress the difficulty** (passive → active
> defender, two-touch → one-touch). That is how five themes carry all the content.

---

### D1 — Ball-mastery basics ✏️ (Coerver: Ball Mastery ✅) · intensity: ease-in
- **Words:** "Both feet," "Try it!"
- **Warm-up (15m):** ball each, free dribble; on "freeze," stop it with the sole.
- **Individual (20m):** in a box — **sole rolls, toe taps, foundations (side-to-side)**, both feet; "count your touches." Cue: **"Both feet."**
- **Group (20m):** King of the Ring — protect your ball, knock others out.
- **Huddle:** "Which surface felt best on your weaker foot?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] mastery · [Ps] confidence · [S] listening · **Smarts:** count touches.

### D2 — Dribbling & change of direction ✏️ (Coerver: Ball Mastery · turns ✅) · intensity: build
_Merges running-with-the-ball and turns; rotate the emphasis across the weeks._
- **Words:** "Head up," "Find space."
- **Warm-up (15m):** "traffic" — everyone dribbles in a grid without bumping; on "freeze," **turn away** (Cruyff / drag-back).
- **Individual (20m):** **dribble through the gates** (most in 60s, no gate twice; a **left-foot-only** round) **↔ inside / outside / sole cuts** to change direction on a call; both feet; add a light "shark."
- **Group (20m):** Sharks & Minnows → **1v1 to gates** (beat the defender by changing direction).
- **Huddle:** "How did you find space — or beat someone with a turn?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] close control + turns · [P] agility, decel/re-accel · **Smarts:** count gates.

### D3 — 1v1 & the body feint ✏️ (Coerver: 1v1 intro ✅) · intensity: **PEAK**
- **Words:** "Try it!"
- **Warm-up (15m):** free dribble; practice a **body feint** (drop the shoulder).
- **Individual (20m):** **body feint → accelerate past**; then **step-over / scissors** for those ready; both sides, at a cone/passive defender.
- **Group (20m):** 1v1 to goal/gates — try a move each turn (no penalty for failing).
- **Huddle:** "Did the defender buy your fake?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] feints · [Ps] courage to fail · [S] encouraging others.

### D4 — First touch (intro receiving) ✏️ (Coerver: Receiving ✅) · intensity: recover
- **Words:** "First touch out of your feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** pairs, one ball — gentle rolls to each other, cushion & stop.
- **Individual (20m):** receive a rolled pass and take a **first touch into space** (to a cone), either foot. Cue: **"check your shoulders"** before you receive.
- **Group (20m):** 2v2 tiny game — reward a good first touch.
- **Huddle:** "Where did your first touch go — into space or stuck?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] first touch · [Ps] focus · [S] working with a partner.

### D5 — Creativity, play & checkpoint ✏️ · intensity: light
- **Words:** "Try it!," "Head up."
- **Warm-up (15m):** free "show your favourite move."
- **Individual (20m):** **invent-a-move** station + revisit sole rolls / cuts / feints (child's choice = autonomy).
- **Group (20m):** small-sided **festival** — just play; celebrate tries.
- **Huddle + checkpoint:** quick [assessment](../03-assessment.md) note per child (enjoys the ball? head-up? tries a move?).
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; **Smarts:** each child names their move.
