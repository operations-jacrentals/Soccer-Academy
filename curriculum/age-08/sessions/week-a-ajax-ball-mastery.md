# Age 8 · Week A — AJAX · Ball Mastery ("Me & the Ball") · day library

**Week 1 of the repeating [3-week cycle](../01-cycle-plan.md)** — five day-plans
(**D1–D5**) that come back around **every loop (~13× a year)**. Academy identity:
**Ajax** — technique & creativity ([research](../../research/ajax-de-toekomst-model.md)).
Coerver pyramid blocks: **Ball Mastery + 1v1** (+ intro receiving).

> **Provenance:** the **progression** (Coerver Ball Mastery → the pyramid) and the
> **technique-first, max-touch** frame are ✅ **sourced**
> ([references](../../references/sources.md)); the **academy identity** is ✅
> researched. The **specific activities, counts, and the re-sequenced day order**
> are ✏️ **coaching judgment**, drawing on the
> [drill catalog](../../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md). Every day-plan
> uses the [Warm-up 15 → Individual 15 → Group 15 → PR Time 10 → Huddle 5 shape](../02-session-template.md)
> (the **day's focus is the Individual block**) and is **run ×3 that day** (3
> attendance ticks); a **ball each**, ~60 min ✏️, **no standings** (KNVB ✅).
>
> **The cycle loops.** A day-type runs ~13 times across the year, so **rotate the
> specific drill inside each theme loop to loop** (e.g. gates one loop, a
> Cruyff-turn circuit the next — **family-locked**: keep the swap in the day's
> skill family) and **level up the difficulty** (passive → active defender,
> two-touch → one-touch). That is how five themes carry a third of the year.
> **PR Time** closes each session: the day's three measures vs the child's own
> best — a **single day-PR per measure, only climbs** →
> [reports & card](../04-reports-and-player-profile.md).

### D1 — Ball-mastery basics ✏️ (Coerver: Ball Mastery ✅) · intensity: ease-in
- **Words:** "Both feet," "Try it!"
- **Warm-up (15m):** ball each, free dribble; on "freeze," stop it with the sole.
- **Individual (20m):** in a box — **sole rolls, toe taps, foundations (side-to-side)**, both feet; "count your touches." Cue: **"Both feet."**
- **Group (20m):** King of the Ring — protect your ball, knock others out *(never scored — elimination)*.
- **Huddle:** "Which surface felt best on your weaker foot?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Touch Count 30s (both feet) · Toe-Taps 30s · Figure-8 weave /45s.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] mastery · [Ps] confidence · [S] listening · **Smarts:** count touches.

### D2 — Dribbling & change of direction ✏️ (Coerver: Ball Mastery · turns ✅) · intensity: build
_Merges running-with-the-ball and turns; rotate the emphasis across the loops._
- **Words:** "Head up," "Find space."
- **Warm-up (15m):** "traffic" — everyone dribbles in a grid without bumping; on "freeze," **turn away** (Cruyff / drag-back).
- **Individual (20m):** **dribble through the gates** (most in 60s, no gate twice; a **weak-foot-only** round) **↔ inside / outside / sole cuts** to change direction on a call; both feet; add a light "shark."
- **Group (20m):** Sharks & Minnows → **1v1 to gates** (beat the defender by changing direction).
- **Huddle:** "How did you find space — or beat someone with a turn?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Gate Run 60s · Weak-foot Gate Run 60s · Slalom time (fastest **clean** run).
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] close control + turns · [P] agility, decel/re-accel · **Smarts:** count gates.

### D3 — 1v1 & the body feint ✏️ (Coerver: 1v1 ✅) · intensity: **PEAK**
- **Words:** "Try it!"
- **Warm-up (15m):** free dribble; practice a **body feint** (drop the shoulder).
- **Individual (20m):** **body feint → accelerate past**; then **step-over / scissors / drag-back** for those ready; both sides, at a cone → passive → active defender (level up loop over loop).
- **Group (20m):** 1v1 to goal/gates — try a move each turn (no penalty for failing).
- **Huddle:** "Did the defender buy your fake?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Beat-the-defender /10 · Move-gauntlet time · **Brave Tries → 🦁 lion** (attempts count, success doesn't have to).
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] feints · [Ps] courage to fail · [S] encouraging others.

### D4 — First touch into dribble ✏️ (Coerver: Receiving ✅) · intensity: recover
- **Words:** "First touch out of your feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** pairs, one ball — gentle rolls to each other, cushion & stop.
- **Individual (20m):** receive a rolled pass and take a **first touch into space** (to a cone), either foot — then **go**: trap-and-dribble away. Cue: **"check your shoulders"** before you receive.
- **Group (20m):** 2v2 tiny game — reward a good first touch.
- **Huddle:** "Where did your first touch go — into space or stuck?"
- **PR Time ✏️:** Cushion Count 60s · Wall-touch 60s · Trap-and-go time.
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] first touch · [Ps] focus · [S] working with a partner.

### D5 — Creativity & skill-move showcase ✏️ · intensity: light · *checkpoint*
- **Words:** "Try it!," "Head up."
- **Warm-up (15m):** free "show your favourite move."
- **Individual (20m):** **invent-a-move** station + revisit sole rolls / cuts / feints / step-overs (child's choice = autonomy); build the **showcase** — each child performs their move.
- **Group (20m):** small-sided **festival** — just play; celebrate tries; **My-Move shows** in the game.
- **Huddle + checkpoint:** quick [assessment](../03-assessment.md) note per child (enjoys the ball? head-up? tries a move?).
- **PR Time ✏️:** Trick Menu /5 (variety) · Juggling PB · My-Move shows in game — **My-Move attempts → 🦁 lion**.
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; **Smarts:** each child names their move.
