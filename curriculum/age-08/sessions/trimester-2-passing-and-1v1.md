# Age 8 · Trimester 2 — Passing & 1v1 (day library)

A **5-day circuit** (D6–D10) for **Trimester 2** of the [trimester plan](../01-trimester-plan.md) —
one week's worth of day-types that **repeats each week** across the ~13-week trimester.
Coerver pyramid blocks: **Receiving & Passing** + **1v1 Moves**. Theme: *"Me, the
Ball & a Friend."*

> **Provenance:** same as [Trimester 1](trimester-1-ball-mastery.md) — Coerver progression &
> technique-first are ✅ sourced; specific activities/order are ✏️ coaching
> judgment on the [drill catalog](../../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md).
> Warm-up → Individual → Group (the warm-up is now a **rondo** most days), **run ×3
> that day**; ball each / one per pair, no standings. **The circuit repeats weekly** —
> rotate the drill inside each theme and **progress the difficulty** across the ~13 weeks.

---

### D6 — Inside-foot passing ✏️ (Coerver: Passing ✅) · intensity: ease-in
- **Words:** "Both feet."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **3v1 rondo.**
- **Individual (20m):** **inside-foot pass** to a partner/gate; weight & accuracy; **both feet**; two-touch → one-touch; count completions (smarts).
- **Group (20m):** passing gates game — score by passing through cones to a teammate.
- **Huddle:** "Was your pass too soft, too hard, or just right?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] passing · [S] cooperation · **Smarts:** count/score passes.

### D7 — Give-and-go (1‑2) ✏️ · intensity: build
- **Words:** "Pass and move."
- **Warm-up (15m):** **4v1 rondo.**
- **Individual (20m):** **wall pass / give-and-go** around a cone "defender"; pass, move, receive.
- **Group (20m):** 3v1 mini-rondo → 2v2 with a target; reward a 1‑2.
- **Huddle:** "What did you do right after you passed?" (move!)
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] combination · [Ps] anticipation · [S] teamwork.

### D8 — 1v1 moves: step-over & drag-back ✏️ (Coerver: 1v1 ✅) · intensity: **PEAK**
- **Words:** "Try it!"
- **Warm-up (15m):** free dribble; practice **step-over** and **drag-back**.
- **Individual (20m):** move → accelerate away; both feet; vs. a passive then active defender.
- **Group (20m):** 1v1 to goal — pick a move.
- **Huddle:** "Which move is becoming your favourite?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] feints · [P] change of pace · [Ps] courage.

### D9 — Receiving on the half-turn + scanning ✏️ (Coerver: Receiving ✅) · intensity: recover
- **Words:** "Check your shoulders."
- **Warm-up (15m):** "scan when you can" — receive & turn, open body; spot who has a ball.
- **Individual (20m):** **receive on the half-turn** to play forward; **scan** before receiving (coach flashes a number behind them = smarts).
- **Group (20m):** 4v2 keep-ball — turn and find the free player.
- **Huddle:** "What did you see before the ball came?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] receiving · [Ps] scanning/awareness · **Smarts:** read the number.

### D10 — Finishing & combine + checkpoint ✏️ (Coerver: Finishing → Group Play ✅) · intensity: light-moderate
_Merges finishing technique and the pass–move–finish pattern; rotate across the weeks._
- **Words:** "Head up, pick your spot," "Next play."
- **Warm-up (15m):** dribble & shoot at mini-goals.
- **Individual (20m):** **side-foot placement** up close → **laces** with a short run-up; both feet; then the pattern **pass → 1‑2 → finish** (unopposed → light defender).
- **Group (20m):** small-sided game — look for a 1‑2 before a shot; celebrate composed finishes over blasts.
- **Huddle + checkpoint:** [assessment](../03-assessment.md) — plays a 1‑2? tries moves in games? finishes composed? shares?
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; "Next play" after a miss; **Smarts:** which corner (spatial).
