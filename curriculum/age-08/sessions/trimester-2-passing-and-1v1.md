# Age 8 · Trimester 2 — Passing & 1v1 (session library)

Six runnable sessions for **Trimester 2** of the [trimester plan](../01-trimester-plan.md).
Coerver pyramid blocks: **Receiving & Passing** + **1v1 Moves**. Theme: *"Me, the
Ball & a Friend."*

> **Provenance:** same as [Trimester 1](trimester-1-ball-mastery.md) — Coerver progression &
> technique-first are ✅ sourced; specific activities/order are ✏️ coaching
> judgment on the [drill catalog](../../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md).
> Rondo → Individual → Group, ball each / one per pair, no standings.

---

### S7 — Inside-foot passing ✏️ (Coerver: Passing ✅)
- **Words:** "Both feet."
- **Warm-up (5m):** pairs, one ball — push-pass back and forth, cushion each one.
- **Skill (20m):** **inside-foot pass** to a partner/gate; weight & accuracy; **both feet**; count completions (smarts).
- **Game (15m):** passing gates game — score by passing through cones to a teammate.
- **Huddle:** "Was your pass too soft, too hard, or just right?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] passing · [S] cooperation · **Smarts:** count/score passes.

### S8 — Give-and-go (1‑2) ✏️
- **Words:** "Pass and move."
- **Warm-up (5m):** pairs pass while walking, then jogging.
- **Skill (20m):** **wall pass / give-and-go** around a cone "defender"; pass, move, receive.
- **Game (15m):** 3v1 mini-rondo → 2v2 with a target; reward a 1‑2.
- **Huddle:** "What did you do right after you passed?" (move!)
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] combination · [Ps] anticipation · [S] teamwork.

### S9 — Receiving on the half-turn + scanning ✏️ (Coerver: Receiving ✅)
- **Words:** "Check your shoulders."
- **Warm-up (5m):** receive & turn, open body.
- **Skill (20m):** **receive on the half-turn** to play forward; **scan** before receiving (coach flashes a number behind them = smarts).
- **Game (15m):** 4v2 keep-ball — turn and find the free player.
- **Huddle:** "What did you see before the ball came?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] receiving · [Ps] scanning/awareness · **Smarts:** read the number.

### S10 — 1v1 moves: step-over & drag-back ✏️ (Coerver: 1v1 ✅)
- **Words:** "Try it!"
- **Warm-up (5m):** practice **step-over** and **drag-back** free.
- **Skill (20m):** move → accelerate away; both feet; vs. a passive then active defender.
- **Game (15m):** 1v1 to goal — pick a move.
- **Huddle:** "Which move is becoming your favourite?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] feints · [P] change of pace · [Ps] courage.

### S11 — Finishing: side-foot & laces ✏️ (Coerver: Finishing ✅)
- **Words:** "Head up, pick your spot."
- **Warm-up (5m):** dribble & shoot at mini-goals.
- **Skill (20m):** **side-foot placement** up close → **laces** with a short run-up; both feet; aim for target zones.
- **Game (15m):** 2v2 to goals; celebrate composed finishes over blasts.
- **Huddle:** "Placement or power — which scored more?"
- **Corners/Smarts:** [T] finishing · [Ps] composure · **Smarts:** which corner (spatial).

### S12 — Combine: pass–move–finish ✏️
- **Words:** "Pass and move," "Next play."
- **Warm-up (5m):** free small-sided.
- **Skill (15m):** pattern: **pass → 1‑2 → finish** (unopposed → light defender).
- **Game (20m):** small-sided game; look for a 1‑2 before a shot.
- **Huddle + checkpoint:** [assessment](../03-assessment.md) — plays a 1‑2? tries moves in games? shares?
- **Corners/Smarts:** all four; "Next play" after a miss.
