# Age 8 — The Repeating 3-Week Cycle (Ajax · La Masia · Benfica)

How the [age-8 targets](README.md) spread across the year — not as terms, but as
**one 3-week cycle, repeated ~13 times**. Read with the [birds-eye TOC](README.md),
the [Documentation Standard](../DOC_STANDARD.md), and the
[interactive tracker spec](interactive/DESIGN-SPEC.md) (the authoritative day map).

> **Provenance:** the **spine** is the **Coerver ball-mastery pyramid** ✅ and the
> **RFEF** sequencing rule *"technique first, collective concepts later"* ✅; the
> **environment** is futsal-style small-sided (**futsal / KNVB**) ✅; the **academy
> identities** (Ajax · La Masia · Benfica) are ✅ researched models. The **3-week
> split, the week→academy assignment, and the D1–D15 day order** are ✏️
> **coaching judgment** — a re-sequencing of the same sourced material. Sources:
> [references](../references/sources.md) · [research/](../research/README.md).

## The year, as a loop ✏️

**~40 training weeks ≈ ~13 loops of one 3-week cycle** (15 day-plans × ~13 loops
≈ ~195 training days). The cycle — **Weeks A/B/C = days D1–D15** — is the unit
that repeats: nothing new is bolted on mid-year; the *same* fifteen days come back
around and **level up**. Each day is **one 60-min plan** ([session
template](02-session-template.md)) **run in all 3 of that day's sessions** (3
attendance ticks per day; 1 tick = 1 hour). Intensity builds then recovers across
each week (**tactical periodization** ✅ — the exact pattern ✏️): **Mon ease-in ·
Tue build · Wed peak · Thu recover · Fri light.** The cycle follows the Coerver
pyramid, not the school calendar — difficulty individualizes per child, the rhythm
stays shared.

| Week | Academy identity | Focus | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **AJAX** — *"Me & the Ball"* | technique & creativity — ball mastery, dribbling, 1v1 | [D1–D5](sessions/week-a-ajax-ball-mastery.md) |
| **B** | **LA MASIA** — *"Me, the Ball & a Friend"* | position & passing — rondo, receiving, scanning | [D6–D10](sessions/week-b-la-masia-passing.md) |
| **C** | **BENFICA** — *"Playing the Game"* | athlete & finish / compete — finishing, speed, defending, GK, festival | [D11–D15](sessions/week-c-benfica-game.md) |

*(✅ academy models: [Ajax](../research/ajax-de-toekomst-model.md) ·
[La Masia](../research/barcelona-la-masia-model.md) ·
[Benfica](../research/benfica-model.md). ✏️ the A→B→C assignment and day order are
coaching judgment.)*

## How the loops progress ✏️

- **Drills level up each loop** — same theme, harder form: passive → active
  defender, two-touch → one-touch, bigger → smaller space; rotate the specific
  drill inside each day's theme (gates one loop, a Cruyff-turn circuit the next).
  Swaps are **family-locked** — change the drill, keep the skill family →
  [reports & card](04-reports-and-player-profile.md).
- **Emphasis shifts A→C across the year** — early loops lean on Week A (fall in
  love with the ball); by the late loops Week C carries the weight (playing the
  game). The cycle order never changes; the *coaching emphasis inside it* does.
- **The cycle is the Coerver pyramid in miniature** ✅ — Ball Mastery (Week A) →
  Receiving/Passing (Week B) → 1v1 (D3) → Speed (D12) → Finishing (D11) → Group
  Play (Week C); each loop climbs it again, one level up. ✏️ the mapping.
- **Every Friday is a checkpoint** (D5 · D10 · D15) — a light
  [assessment](03-assessment.md) note per child each loop, rolling up into the
  start-of-year / mid-year / before-9 reviews.

## Age-8 planning principles

- **Individual technique first, collective concepts later** ✅ (RFEF *Método Integrado*).
- **Technique runs all year** — the weeks shift *emphasis*, following the **Coerver pyramid** ✅. ✏️
- **Maximum touches** — **futsal-style** small spaces; **one ball per pair**. ✅
- **Street / deliberate play & discovery** ✅ (KNVB); **development over winning — no standings** ✅.
- **Short blocks** — attention is **one task at a time** ✅.

## Week A — AJAX · "Me & the Ball" · Coerver **Ball Mastery + 1v1** ✏️

- **Days:** D1 ball-mastery basics · D2 dribbling & change of direction · D3 **1v1 & the body feint (peak)** · D4 first touch into dribble · D5 creativity & skill-move showcase *(checkpoint)* → [day library](sessions/week-a-ajax-ball-mastery.md).
- **Focus:** ball mastery (both feet), running with the ball, first touch, **1v1 bravery & individual creativity**.
- **Coerver blocks:** Ball Mastery + 1v1 Moves → (intro) Receiving. ✅
- **Blocks:** [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md) (control, first touch), [index/02](../index/02-skill-moves.md) (body feint, step-over, drag-back).
- **Physical:** movement ABCs through games → [index/04](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md).
- **Psych/Social:** enjoyment & confidence; courage to try a move; routines, listening.
- **Smarts:** count touches; left vs. right foot.
- **Words:** "Head up," "Both feet," "Try it!"
- **Signs of growth across the loops ✏️:** enjoys the ball; dribbles in space with some head-up; tries a move without fear.

## Week B — LA MASIA · "Me, the Ball & a Friend" · Coerver **Receiving & Passing** ✏️

- **Days:** D6 inside-foot passing · D7 give-and-go · D8 **rondo (peak)** · D9 receive on the half-turn + scanning · D10 positional keep-ball / find space *(checkpoint)* → [day library](sessions/week-b-la-masia-passing.md).
- **Focus:** passing weight & accuracy (both feet), **the give-and-go**, receiving on the half-turn, scanning, keeping the ball as a group.
- **Coerver blocks:** Receiving & Passing. ✅ The **rondo is the engine** (La Masia ✅).
- **Blocks:** [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md) (passing, receiving), [index/03](../index/03-training-methodology-and-drills.md) (3v1/4v1 rondos, keep-ball).
- **Physical:** change of pace/direction through games.
- **Psych/Social:** sharing, taking turns, cooperation; the group beats *its own* record.
- **Smarts:** simple **dribble-vs-pass** decisions; scan-and-count.
- **Words:** "Pass and move," "Check your shoulders," "Find space."
- **Signs of growth across the loops ✏️:** plays a simple 1‑2; shares the ball; scans before receiving.

## Week C — BENFICA · "Playing the Game" · Coerver **Finishing → Group Play** ✏️

- **Days:** D11 finishing · D12 speed & agility with the ball *(monthly-guarded)* · D13 **defending 1v1 (peak)** · D14 goalkeeping taster · D15 festival, compete & rules *(checkpoint)* → [day library](sessions/week-c-benfica-game.md).
- **Focus:** finishing **placement** (both feet), playful athleticism with the ball, **intro defending (jockey)**, everyone tries **goalkeeping**, futsal-style festival + rules.
- **Coerver blocks:** Finishing + Group Play (individual skill → team play). ✅
- **Blocks:** [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md) (finishing, defending, GK basics), [index/07](../index/07-tactics-and-systems.md) (find space — kept minimal), [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md) (small-sided, rules).
- **Physical:** game-based speed/agility — **maturation-guarded**: D12 logs only a **light monthly personal best**, off the daily flow; **no formal fitness testing at 8** (LTAD ✅).
- **Psych/Social:** teamwork, respect, courage; "Next play."
- **Smarts:** which goal is ours/theirs; in/out of play; spatial awareness.
- **Words:** "Pick your spot," "Stay on your feet," "Next play."
- **Signs of growth across the loops ✏️:** plays small-sided with less clumping; knows core restarts; bounces back from mistakes.

## Notes

- **Not rigid:** a child ahead levels up sooner; a beginner stays longer on the
  gentler forms of the same day — the cycle keeps everyone in the same rhythm
  while difficulty individualizes via each child's **IDP** →
  [assessment](03-assessment.md).
- **Session structure:** every session uses the **Warm-up 15 → Individual 15 →
  Group 15 → PR Time 10 → Huddle 5** shape (warm-up = a rondo by default; the **day's focus is
  the Individual block**) → [session template](02-session-template.md).
- **Measures:** each day carries **three self-referential measures** at PR Time —
  the day's best per measure is kept as a **single PR that only climbs** →
  [reports & card](04-reports-and-player-profile.md) ·
  [tracker spec](interactive/DESIGN-SPEC.md).
- **Cadence** (sessions/week, weeks/year) is a family/scheduling choice ✏️ — total
  contact time and touches matter more than the exact split.
