# Age 8 — Assessment & Individual Development Plan

Light-touch, encouraging, **development-focused** — never ranking. It exists to
guide the next session, not to grade the child.

> **Provenance:** the rubric **frameworks** — the **Four-Corner Model** (FA) and
> **TIPS** (Ajax) — are ✅ **sourced** ([references/sources.md](../references/sources.md)).
> The **rating scale, the specific age-8 checks, and the cadence** are ✏️
> **coaching judgment**. The encouraging tone reflects the sourced fact that U8
> players have a **high need for adult approval** ✅.

## Rating scale ✏️

Three simple levels — *where is this child today?*

- **🌱 Emerging** — beginning to try it, with help.
- **🌿 Developing** — does it sometimes, in practice.
- **🌳 Secure** — does it often, including in games.

*(No numbers, no ranking against other kids. Compare a child only to their own
last check.)*

## Age-8 rubric (Four Corners ✅ / checks ✏️)

| Corner | Age-8 signature checks ✏️ |
|---|---|
| **Technical `[T]`** | dribbles head-up in space · uses **both feet** · cushions a first touch · plays a give-and-go · **tries a move in a game** |
| **Physical `[P]`** | changes direction under control · balance & coordination in games |
| **Psychological `[Ps]`** | **brave to try & make mistakes** · sustains focus on one task · **"next play"** after an error |
| **Social `[S]`** | **shares the ball** · listens & waits a turn · respects teammates/rules · manages own kit |

*(Maps cleanly to **TIPS** ✅ too: Technique / Insight / Personality / Speed.)*

## Cadence ✏️

- **Every Friday** (D5 · D10 · D15 of the [cycle](01-cycle-plan.md)) — a quick
  checkpoint note per child, each loop; these micro-checks feed the reviews below.
- **Start of year** — baseline (which "coming in" targets are already there?).
- **Mid-year** — progress check; adjust the [cycle plan](01-cycle-plan.md)
  emphasis (which week — A, B or C — needs the weight?).
- **Before 9** — year-end; set the handoff to the age-9 plan.

## Individual Development Plan (IDP)

One short page per child (the format the top academies use → [index/11](../index/11-player-development-and-pathways.md)):

- **Strengths** (2–3) — what they already love/do well.
- **Next steps** (1–2 only) — the *smallest* useful targets for the next block.
- **What lights them up** — to keep it fun and intrinsic.
- **Notes** — growth since last check (celebrate it out loud).

> **Auto-drafted from the card.** The [player card](04-reports-and-player-profile.md)
> reads these three straight off the child's own hexagon *shape*: **Strengths** ← the
> longest spokes (`shape.signature`); **Next steps** ← the lowest *technical* spoke
> (`shape.next_quest`, with Pace/Physical maturation-guarded out) + a weaker-foot flag;
> **What lights them up** ← their signature move. The card is self-referential — it never
> ranks child vs. child (see §5.4 no-card-vs-card display rule).

> **Golden rule:** measure to **encourage and guide**, never to pressure or rank.
> Effort, bravery, and joy are the headline metrics at age 8. The card enforces this in
> the schema itself — there is **no overall number and no cross-child field**; the
> day's marks are a **single PR per measure that only climbs**, never a grade.
