# Age 8 — Milestones & Measurement

How we **measure** the [15 day-plans](sessions/trimester-1-ball-mastery.md) (D1–D15).
**V1 is deliberately simple: one binary milestone per day, one checkbox per player.**
Gamification (badges, ladders, PB arcs) is **deferred** — researched below as the
foundation, but **not yet adopted**. Complements (never replaces) the
[Emerging/Developing/Secure assessment](03-assessment.md). Written to the
[Documentation Standard](../DOC_STANDARD.md): every number is **✅ sourced** (with link) or
**✏️ coaching judgment** — nothing invented.

---

## V1 — The milestone checkbox system (what we run now) ✏️

**The mechanic:** a **roster** of players lives at the top of the interactive year
document. It drives two things on every day card:

1. **Milestone checkbox — in the day *row* (always visible).** One checkbox per player
   sits in the day's header strip, so a coach scanning the **collapsed** list of 15 days
   sees at a glance *who still needs what* — the point of the week-end review. The box is
   **checked the first time the child hits the milestone** (in any of the day's three
   sessions) and stays checked. No scores, no levels, no comparisons. The day-type repeats
   ~13 weeks, so an unchecked box just means "not yet."
2. **Attendance — 3 boxes per player, inside the day.** Each day = 3 one-hour sessions;
   the day body carries **S1 · S2 · S3** checkboxes per player. Tick a box for each session
   the child attended. Each ticked box = **1 session = 1 hour**, and every player's chip in
   the roster shows their **running hours total** — so *sessions/hours over time* is tracked
   without the coach recording anything per drill.

_(Prototype note ✏️: state saves per device (browser storage); the 3 attendance boxes
capture one cycle of that day-type — true multi-week hour accumulation across the ~13
repeats is a shared-storage app feature, not built here.)_

**Milestone design rules ✏️:**
- **Binary and observable** — hit it or not yet; a coach (or the child) can call it in the moment.
- **Deliberately small** — an inclusive first rung (research below: no age-8 norms exist
  anywhere authoritative, and the inclusive floor is how the German FA designs its
  youngest badge). Small thresholds get checked *during play*, which keeps the coach
  coaching, not recording.
- **Attempts count on bravery days** — peak days (D3/D8) measure *tries*, not successes;
  a failed body feint checks the box.
- **Child vs. the skill, never child vs. child** — no standings (KNVB ✅).

### The 15 milestones (+1 anytime) ✏️

| Day | Milestone (check when first hit) |
|---|---|
| **D1** Ball-mastery basics | **10 tick-tocks in a row** (both feet) |
| **D2** Dribbling & turns | **Beat 1 player to a gate** |
| **D3** 1v1 & body feint · peak | **Attempted 1 body feint in a game** |
| **D4** First touch | **5 clean first touches in a row** |
| **D5** Creativity & checkpoint | **Showed your own move in the game** |
| **D6** Inside-foot passing | **5 consecutive passes with your partner** |
| **D7** Give-and-go | **Completed 1 give-and-go** |
| **D8** 1v1 moves · peak | **Attempted 1 step-over or drag-back in a game** |
| **D9** Half-turn & scanning | **5 consecutive scans before receiving** |
| **D10** Finishing & combine | **3 on-target finishes in a row** |
| **D11** Finding space | **5-pass team streak in keep-ball** |
| **D12** Rondo | **4-pass rondo streak** (the day-plan's own point rule) |
| **D13** Defending · peak | **1 patient win-back (stayed on feet)** |
| **D14** Goalkeeping taster | **5 clean catches in a row** |
| **D15** Festival, rules & checkpoint | **1 restart done correctly, unprompted** |
| **Anytime** | **Juggled 5×** (drop from hands) |

_All thresholds ✏️ coaching judgment (see the negative finding in the research below —
no authoritative age-8 numeric norms exist to adopt). The milestone **forms** (attempt
counting, consecutive-count challenges, streaks) trace to the sourced practices below._

**How it feeds the rubric:** a milestone checked = evidence for 🌱→🌿 on that skill;
checked *in a game* (D2, D3, D5, D8 are game-milestones by construction) = evidence
toward 🌳 Secure at the [assessment checkpoints](03-assessment.md).

## (a) Research findings

### 1 · Real measurement practices we adopt the *form* of ✅

- **✅ Timed touch counts (30s) are standard Coerver-style practice.** Coerver-method
  session plans instruct players to perform tick-tocks/toe-taps and *"do as many contacts
  as they can in 30 or 40 seconds"*; the "count your taps in 30 seconds and try to beat
  your record each session" format is ubiquitous in ball-mastery training.
  → [SportSessionPlanner — Toca Functional Technical Ball Footwork (Coerver)](https://www.sportsessionplanner.com/s/aP7S/Toca-Functional-Technical-Ball-Footwork.html?interface=en) ·
  [SportSessionPlanner — Ball Mastery 2 (Coerver)](https://www.sportsessionplanner.com/s/7cGV/Ball-Mastery-2.html?interface=en) ·
  [Anytime Soccer Training — toe-tap 30s challenge](https://anytime-soccer.com/perfect-the-toe-tap-outside-touch-combo-move/)
  - ⚠️ One commercial program suggests *"aim for 60–75 touches in 30 seconds"* ([Anytime Soccer Training](https://anytime-soccer.com/perfect-the-toe-tap-outside-touch-combo-move/)).
    This is a **single commercial secondary source, not a norm** — per Rule 1 we do **not**
    adopt it as a standard. It is noted only as evidence that 30s counting is a real practice.
- **✅ Timed gate-dribbles (most gates in 60s, weaker-foot rounds) are a published drill
  form** — already cited in [references/sources.md](../references/sources.md)
  → [SoccerXpert — Dribbling Through Gates race](https://soccerxpert.com/drills/details/dribbling-through-gates-race).
- **✅ Timed passing-pair completions are a published drill form** (pairs rotated on
  45–60s windows; "how many accurate passes in 60 seconds" challenges).
  → [Soccer Coach Weekly — Passing pairs](https://www.soccercoachweekly.net/drills-and-games/drills/passing-pairs) ·
  [Jersey Watch — youth passing drills](https://www.jerseywatch.com/blog/simple-youth-soccer-passing-drills)
- **✅ A real published *passing test* exists — and teaches us a caution.** The
  **Loughborough Soccer Passing Test (LSPT)** (16 timed passes + error penalties) is a
  real, named instrument, but peer-reviewed work found it has *"impractical criterion
  validity in elite youth football"* — i.e., even a validated lab test transfers poorly to
  youth game skill. We therefore measure **inside our own drills**, not with imported test
  batteries. → [Science and Medicine in Football (2017)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02640414.2016.1254810)
- **✅ Attempt-counting / rewarding bravery is a real coaching practice.** Coaches award
  "bravery patches" for brave play regardless of outcome, and youth-coaching guidance says
  *"don't only praise the child who wins; praise the child who tries the skill."*
  → [SoccerHelp — bravery patches](https://www.soccerhelp.com/Soccer_Timid_Scared_Brave_Aggressive_Tough_Players.shtml) ·
  [Soccer Coach Weekly — youth coaching tips](https://www.soccercoachweekly.net/coaching-advice/skills-and-tips-for-successful-youth-soccer-coaching)

### 2 · Real badge/level schemes we model ours on ✅

- **✅ Coerver "99 Skills to Ball Mastery" ladder** — Coerver's own published scheme:
  **Bronze after 30 essential skills → Silver (37 more) → Gold (20) → Master/Black (12)**.
  Progression = *skills collected*, not scores vs. other kids.
  → [Coerver — 99 Skills to Ball Mastery](https://www.coerver.com/blog/ballmastery/) ·
  [playgreatsoccer review](https://playgreatsoccer.com/blog/99-skills-to-ball-mastery-review/) ·
  [Soccer Training Info](https://soccer-training-info.com/coerver-soccer-skills-99-skills-to-ball-mastery/)
- **✅ DFB-Fußball-Abzeichen (German FA skill badge, since 1999)** — a *federation-run*
  skill-badge day: five stations (dribbling *Dribbelkünstler*, short passing *Kurzpass-Ass*,
  crossing, heading, finishing *Elferkönig*) scored into **gold/silver/bronze certificates
  + a pin**. For the youngest (from age ~6 up to E-youth) the **DFB-Paule-Schnupperabzeichen**
  runs **three stations** (dribble, short pass, penalty), **up to 10 points per station —
  and one single point is enough to earn the badge** (a deliberately low floor: everyone
  who tries earns something).
  → [DFB — die Abzeichen](https://www.dfb.de/dfb-fussball-abzeichen/die-abzeichen/) ·
  [DFB organisation brochure (PDF)](https://www.dfb.de/fileadmin/_dfbdam/232116-FUBAZ_ORGA_Broschuere_RZ_lay.pdf) ·
  [fussball.de — alle Infos](https://www.fussball.de/newsdetail/alle-infos-zum-dfb-fussball-abzeichen/-/article-id/215867) ·
  [FV Rheinland summary](https://www.fv-rheinland.de/viel-spass-fuer-alle-altersgruppen-die-wichtigsten-infos-zum-dfb-fussball-abzeichen/)
  - **Design lesson we adopt:** badge *architecture* ✅ (stations → badge; inclusive floor).
    **We do not adopt its point bands** — banded scoring is normative comparison, which our
    ethos excludes. ✏️
- **✅ US club "Juggling Club" milestone ladders are real and widespread** — e.g., wristband
  rungs at **10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 / 1000** juggles (CDO Soccer, "like earning
  belts in karate"), badge tiers at **10/20/35/50/75/100/150** (Sun Prairie).
  → [CDO Soccer Juggling Club](https://clubs.bluesombrero.com/Default.aspx?tabid=244904) ·
  [Sun Prairie SC Juggling Challenge](https://www.sunprairiesoccer.com/jugglingchallenge) ·
  [Quakertown SC 100+ Juggles Club](https://www.quakertownsoccerclub.org/100-juggles-club) ·
  [MYSL Surf Juggling Club](https://myslsoccer.org/juggling-club)
- **⚠️ The only age-8 juggling "standard" found is club-level, not federation:** Franklin
  Youth Soccer's U8 priority — drop from hands, **4+ touches using 2+ surfaces**.
  Single club source → not adopted as a norm.
  → [Franklin Youth Soccer — U8 skill priorities](https://www.franklinyouthsoccer.org/resources/skill-priorities-overview/skill-priorities-u8/)
- **✅ KNVB offers participation diplomas, not graded youth skill scores** (e.g., the
  *voetbaldiploma* in its Dromen-van-Oranje program) — consistent with its no-standings
  philosophy already sourced in [references/sources.md](../references/sources.md).
  → [KNVB — voorbeeld diploma](https://knvb.maglr.com/dromen-van-oranje/voorbeeld-diploma)

### 3 · The psychology: why self-referenced, process-first metrics ✅

- **✅ Goal-setting theory (Locke & Latham) is real and extends to sport** — Locke & Latham
  (1985) explicitly proposed the extension; systematic reviews confirm goal-setting
  interventions work in sport but find **no definitive winner among goal *types*** —
  so the choice of process/PB goals for 8-year-olds is made on the child-development
  evidence below, not on GST alone.
  → [Healy et al. 2021 — GST in sport, systematic review](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1750984X.2021.1901298) ·
  [Lochbaum et al. 2022 — goal setting in sport, meta-analysis](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1750984X.2022.2116723)
- **✅ Mastery (task-involving) motivational climate — Ames (1992)** — a climate that
  emphasizes **effort, skill mastery, and improvement against self-referenced criteria**
  (vs. an ego climate of social comparison) is consistently associated with **enjoyment,
  intrinsic motivation, perceived competence — and better motor-skill development** in
  children randomized to mastery-oriented programs.
  → [Task-involving climate & enjoyment in youth footballers (PMC)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9966829/) ·
  [Motivational climate — overview (ScienceDirect)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/psychology/motivational-climate) ·
  [Peer motivational climate scoping review (Frontiers)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1635666/full)
- **✅ Personal Best (PB) goals are a named, published framework** (Andrew Martin, UNSW):
  *"specific, challenging, competitively **self-referenced** targets to match or exceed a
  previous best."* Evidence: PB goals predict higher engagement and achievement gains
  (Martin & Elliot 2016 found a causal effect), with the **largest benefits for
  lower-achieving children**. (Domain is education; the transfer to our training metrics
  is ✏️ our application of a ✅ framework.)
  → [UNSW newsroom — PB goals research](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2021/07/setting--personal-best--goals-helps-students---especially-those-) ·
  [Martin — PB goal setting & achievement gains](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/290306540_The_role_of_personal_best_PB_goal_setting_in_students'_academic_achievement_gains)
- **✅ Fun is the #1 reason children play — and quit** (Visek's FUN MAPS): ~9 in 10 kids say
  they play because it's fun; the study mapped **81 fun-determinants in 11 fun-factors**,
  the top factor being **"Trying Hard"** — while **winning ranked ~#40**. ~70% of kids quit
  organized sport by ~13, chiefly because it stops being fun. Our headline metrics
  (effort, bravery, tries) *are* the top fun-determinant.
  → [GW Today — FUN MAPS](https://gwtoday.gwu.edu/new-study-dispels-myths-about-what-makes-youth-sports-fun) ·
  [ScienceDaily summary](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2019/11/191114100903.htm) ·
  [Changing the Game Project — why kids quit](https://changingthegameproject.com/why-kids-quit-sports/)
- **✅ Spacing practice across a day is evidence-backed** — Donovan & Radosevich's 1999
  meta-analysis (*J. Applied Psychology* 84(5):795–805; 63 studies): **distributed beats
  massed practice** overall (d ≈ 0.46), strongest for simple motor skills (d ≈ 0.97).
  This supports the 3-sessions-with-recovery structure the day metric rides on. (The
  specific "AM mark → PM best" *measurement arc* is ✏️ our design on top of it.)
  → [Donovan & Radosevich 1999 (PDF)](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/spaced-repetition/1999-donovan.pdf)

### 4 · The decisive negative finding

**No federation or peer-reviewed source publishes numeric benchmarks for 8-year-olds** on
touch counts, gate-dribble times, or pass completions. Talent-ID dribble/pass test norms
exist only for older ages (e.g., U9→U17 athleticism batteries; dribble-skill determinant
studies at 12–19), and even those are validity-contested for youth
([Frontiers U9→U17 study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2021.625645/full) ·
[dribbling determinants](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/309265177_Dribbling_skill_determinants_in_youth_soccer_players) ·
[LSPT critique](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02640414.2016.1254810)).
**Per Rule 1, we therefore publish no "typical 8-year-old" numbers.** Every benchmark
below is the child's own previous mark. (Rule 2 — "pick the higher standard" — is
inapplicable to norms that don't exist; where only commercial numbers exist, Rule 1 wins
and we decline them.)

## (b) DEFERRED — the personal-best day arc (not yet adopted)

### One number per day, updated across the three sessions ✏️

Each day-type D1–D15 has **one primary day metric** — a number ONE coach (or the child
themselves — the "count your touches" Smarts thread is already in the
[day plans](sessions/trimester-1-ball-mastery.md)) can capture with cones, balls, and a
stopwatch. **The coach never records per-session stats.** The day card holds:

| Field | What it is | When written |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning Mark** | the metric from **session 1's** measured window | end of session 1 |
| **Evening Best** | best of sessions 2–3 (only updated **if beaten**) | end of sessions 2/3 |
| **Day Gain (Δ)** | Evening Best − Morning Mark — **the same-day-mastery signal** | evening huddle |
| **Circuit PB?** | did today's best beat this day-type's best from previous weeks? | evening huddle |

**The arc:** session 1 *sets the mark* → sessions 2–3 *chase it* → the evening huddle
compares the child **(a)** to their morning self (Day Gain — did today's repetition turn
into same-day mastery?) and **(b)** to their last week's self (Circuit PB — is the
13-week curve rising?). Both comparisons are self-referenced (PB goals ✅, mastery
climate ✅); neither compares child to child (KNVB ✅).

### The anchor-protocol rule ✏️ (what makes 13 weeks comparable)

The circuit repeats weekly and **drills rotate inside each theme** — so the *measured
window* must not rotate with them. Each day-type gets a fixed **anchor protocol**: the
same ~2–3 minutes at the end of the Individual block (or a fixed count-window in the
Group game), with **identical setup all trimester** — same box/gate size and spacing, same
distance, same window length, same counting rule. STEP progressions (passive → active
defender, two-touch → one-touch) apply to the *drill around* the anchor, not to the anchor
itself. If an anchor setup must change, the PB history **restarts as a new level**
(celebrated as leveling up, never as losing a record).

### Counting rules ✏️

- **Children count out loud** where possible (self-counting *is* the Smarts curriculum);
  the coach spot-checks and records once per session at most.
- **Attempt counts** (the bravery metrics) count **tries, not successes** — a failed
  step-over counts. Success rates are *never* computed on bravery metrics.
- **Cooperative counts** (pair pass counts, team rondo streaks) are the group vs. its own
  record — allowed; child-vs-child comparison is not.
- **A dip is data, never a penalty.** Tired evening legs, a braver opponent, a harder
  variant — all normal. The rubric levels (🌱🌿🌳) can only move up between checkpoints.

### Load on the coach

≤ 1 primary number/child/day written twice (morning, evening) + optional micro-metric
tallies at the huddle = **~15 primary numbers per child per week**, one index card each.

| **D11 Finding space** | **Team Streak** — most consecutive passes in keep-ball (3v1/4v2) — the *group's* record | best streak in a fixed 3-min window; whole group counts aloud | Group 4v4: passes-to-the-free-player counted | **PB-team** (cooperative — no child ranking) | **Team Streak board** (group vs. its own best) · "Find Space" word badge |
| **D12 Rondo** | **Rondo Streak** — best consecutive-pass streak in the 3v1 rondo | fixed rondo size; the plan's own "4 passes = a point" rule marks the club threshold ✏️ | Ind: one-touch passes *tried* (Attempt) | **PB-team** | "4-Pass Club" · Team Streak board |
| **D13 Defending · PEAK** | **Jockey Clock** — seconds the defender delays the attacker (on feet, no diving in) before a poke or escape; best of 3, cap 10s ✏️ | 1v1 channel, coach stopwatch; same channel all trimester | Group: win-backs counted (effort count) | **PB-self** (simple time trial — longer = better; patience is the process) | "Stay On Your Feet" word badge · Patience patch |
| **D14 Goalkeeping taster** | **Keeper's Ten** — clean W-catches/scoops out of 10 rolled/soft-tossed balls | fixed 10-ball set; everyone takes a turn | Group: brave saves *attempted* in the game (Attempt) | **PB-self** (completion count) | "Brave Hands" badge — earned by *completing the set at all* (inclusive floor modeled on DFB's one-point-earns-the-badge ✅ [Schnupperabzeichen](https://www.dfb.de/dfb-fussball-abzeichen/die-abzeichen/)) |
| **D15 Festival, rules & checkpoint** | **Rules Five /5** — restarts/calls performed correctly unprompted in the festival: throw-in · kick-off · goal restart · corner · in/out call | coach checks off naturally occurring moments during the festival | Huddle: each child names one thing they're proud of | **PB-self** (mastery checklist) | "Game Ready" badge · year-end checkpoint feeds the **Group Play pyramid block** |

**Balance check ✏️:** 5 of 15 primary metrics are pure **attempt/bravery counts** (D3, D5,
D8 + attempt micro-metrics on six other days), 3 are **cooperative team records** (D11,
D12, D6/D7 pairs), and the rest are self-referenced completion counts/time trials — a
process-heavy mix appropriate to the mastery-climate evidence ✅ and the ethos. Peak days
(D3, D8, D13) deliberately carry the *process* metrics so the highest-intensity days are
judged on courage, not outcomes.

## (d) DEFERRED — the gamification layer: levels, badges, boards (not yet adopted)

Vocabulary reuses what already exists: **🌱 Emerging / 🌿 Developing / 🌳 Secure**
([assessment](03-assessment.md)), the **Coerver pyramid blocks**
([trimester plan](01-trimester-plan.md)), and the **words of the day**. Everything below is
✏️ coaching judgment in structure, modeled on the ✅ sourced schemes noted.

### Levels (existing rubric — the metrics feed it, never replace it) ✏️

- **🌱 Emerging** — the child has a baseline on the metric (first Morning Mark recorded).
- **🌿 Developing** — the child has set **Circuit PBs in ≥3 different weeks** on that
  day-type (practice-level growth).
- **🌳 Secure** — the skill shows up **in games**: the day-type's *attempt/game
  micro-metric* is non-zero week after week (e.g., turns taken on in the D2 game, 1-2s
  tried in the D7 game). *Games evidence gates the top level* — matching the rubric's own
  "does it often, including in games."

### Badges

| Badge | Earned by | Modeled on |
|---|---|---|
| **⭐ Evening Beat** | Evening Best > Morning Mark — the **same-day-mastery badge**; earnable every day, announced at the evening huddle (feeds the sourced need for adult approval ✅) | PB goals ✅ ([Martin](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2021/07/setting--personal-best--goals-helps-students---especially-those-)) |
| **🏅 Circuit PB** | today's best beats your best-ever on this day-type | club PB practice ✅ |
| **🦁 Brave Try ladder** | cumulative career *tries* across D3/D8 + game attempt-counts: rungs at **10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 250** tries ✏️ | rung spacing modeled on real juggling-club ladders ✅ ([CDO](https://clubs.bluesombrero.com/Default.aspx?tabid=244904), [Sun Prairie](https://www.sunprairiesoccer.com/jugglingchallenge)); bravery patches ✅ ([SoccerHelp](https://www.soccerhelp.com/Soccer_Timid_Scared_Brave_Aggressive_Tough_Players.shtml)) |
| **📇 Move Collection** | a stamp per distinct move *attempted in a game* (body feint, step-over, drag-back, Cruyff, scissors, own invention…) | Coerver skill-collection ladder ✅ ([99 Skills](https://www.coerver.com/blog/ballmastery/)) |
| **Word badges** | coach-awarded at the huddle for living a word of the day: **"Try It!" · "Both Feet" · "Head Up" · "Find Space" · "Next Play"** (Next Play = kept playing right after a mistake) | words of the year (existing ✏️) |
| **🧱 Pyramid blocks** | trimester completion: **Ball Mastery Block** (D5 final checkpoint) → **Passing & 1v1 Block** (D10) → **Group Play Block** (D15); earned via the checkpoint rubric + a PB history on ≥3 of the trimester's 5 day-types ✏️ | Coerver Bronze→Silver→Gold ✅ · DFB station-badge architecture ✅ |
| **🤝 Team records** | Team Streak / 4-Pass Club — the *group* beats the *group's* own record | cooperative, ethos-safe by construction |

**Inclusive floor (from the DFB design ✅):** every badge scheme has a rung a beginner
earns quickly (first baseline = 🌱 recorded; completing Keeper's Ten at all = Brave Hands;
one try = first Brave Try tally). Badges are **never revoked**, numbers never read out as
comparisons, and a stalled metric changes *the coach's plan* (STEP down), not the child's
status.

### Personal-best boards — never leaderboards

Each child owns a **"My Records" card**: 5 rows (this trimester's day-types) × 13 weekly
columns + a badge strip. The wall board shows **one card per child side by side — rows
are the child's own history, so there is nothing to rank**; plus one shared **Team
Streak** panel. No standings, no cross-child columns, ever (KNVB ✅). The 13-week row *is*
the trimester story a parent sees: Morning Marks rising, Evening Beats accumulating,
blocks earned — and it hands the [IDP](03-assessment.md) its evidence.
