## Where age 8 sits

- **LTAD:** the **FUNdamentals → Learn-to-Train** edge — the on-ramp to the "golden age." → [index/11](../index/11-player-development-and-pathways.md)
- **Academy-model stage:** Ajax **Stage 2 (7–9)** — *technique & creativity*; **"creators, not tacticians."** → [research/ajax](../research/ajax-de-toekomst-model.md)
- **One-line goal for the year:** *fall in love with the ball, and become a brave, two-footed dribbler who tries things.*

## How we teach it (the method layer, every session)

- **Development over winning** · **maximum ball touches** · **a ball each** wherever possible.
- **Games, not lines** — small-sided games (up to 4v4), dribbling games, mini-rondos (3v1).
- **Fun first** — enjoyment is the engine at this age.
- **Whole child** — every session carries a **[T] [P] [Ps] [S]** thread + a "smarts" tie-in.
- **Format:** 4v4, **no fixed goalkeeper**, **ball size 3**. → [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md)
- **Words of the year** (say them constantly): **"Head up" · "Both feet" · "Try it!" · "Find space" · "Next play."** → [index/13](../index/13-teaching-cues-and-acronyms.md)

> **Reading the tables:** *Coming in* = what a typical 8-year-old with prior play
> is already expected to do (the FUN-stage floor). *Before 9* = our projected
> target by year-end. For a **true beginner**, treat the "Coming in" column as the
> first-term target and progress from there.

## 1 · Technical `[T]`

| Topic | Coming in (already expected at 8) | Before 9 (projected) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ball mastery / dribbling** | runs with the ball, frequent touches in open space; stops/starts; dominant foot | changes direction with inside/outside/sole; **both feet** more even; keeps ball close in a crowd; head comes up at times |
| **First touch / receiving** | stops a rolling ball with the inside of the foot | cushions a firmer pass; **first touch into space**; receives with either foot; begins to **"check shoulders"** before receiving |
| **Passing** | short inside-foot push pass to a near target, dominant foot | accurate inside-foot pass ~5–8 m with the right weight; passes with **both feet**; first **give-and-go (1‑2)**; passes to a moving teammate |
| **Finishing** | kicks the ball at goal (toe/laces mix) | **side-foot** finish for placement up close; **laces** drive with a short run-up; shoots with either foot; "hit the target" |
| **Skill moves / 1v1 creativity** | tries to run past a defender; improvises | performs 2–3 foundational moves (**body feint, step-over, drag-back, stop-and-go**) in practice and *attempts them in games* | 
| **Intro defending** | chases the ball to win it | begins to **jockey** (stay on feet, don't dive in); pokes the ball away; simple **goal-side** idea |
| **Intro goalkeeping** | picks the ball up, throws it anyhow | everyone tries GK: **ready position**, scoop/catch a rolling ball, roll/throw it out |

→ Building blocks: [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md), [index/02](../index/02-skill-moves.md)

## 2 · Physical `[P]`

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Movement skills (ABCs)** | runs, jumps, hops, skips; changes direction awkwardly | **agility, balance, coordination** improved through games; smoother decelerate-and-turn; controlled hop/land |
| **Physical literacy** | short bursts of active play | sustains active play across a session; better whole-body control — **all through games, no formal fitness** |

→ Building blocks: [index/04](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md)

## 3 · Psychological `[Ps]`

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment & confidence** | enjoys playing; can be shy/hesitant | eager and **brave to try things and make mistakes**; attempts 1v1s and moves without fear |
| **Focus & effort** | short attention span; wanders | holds focus for short activities; follows 1–2 step instructions; **effort is valued and shown** |
| **Coping with mistakes** | gets frustrated/upset by errors | **"Next play"** mindset — bounces back; understands mistakes are how we learn |

→ Building blocks: [index/05](../index/05-psychology-and-mental.md)

## 4 · Social / Character `[S]`

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Teamwork & sharing** | "beehive" — everyone chases the ball; reluctant to share | begins to **pass/share** and **find space**; plays *with* teammates, takes turns |
| **Listening & respect** | needs reminders to listen; excited/distracted | listens to the coach, waits a turn, **respects teammates, opponents, and rules** |
| **Responsibility & habits** | needs help with kit/water | manages own **kit and water**; helps set up / pack up; arrives ready |

→ Building blocks: [index/05](../index/05-psychology-and-mental.md), [index/11](../index/11-player-development-and-pathways.md)

## 5 · Game understanding

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Playing 4v4** | follows the ball in a clump; scores any way | begins to **spread out / find space**; knows **which goal is ours/theirs**; less clumping |
| **Rules & format** | knows "score in the goal" | knows **throw-in, in/out of play, no hands (except when trying GK)**, restart basics; plays 4v4, ball size 3 |

→ Building blocks: [index/07](../index/07-tactics-and-systems.md), [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md)

## 6 · Smarts (soccer + smarts)

The Academy's signature thread — soccer as a vehicle for thinking and learning.

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision-making / thinking** | reacts on instinct | makes simple choices (**dribble vs. pass**) with prompting; **"head up / look"** habit forming; scanning introduced |
| **Academic bridge** | counts, knows colors, follows simple instructions | **counts touches/reps**, uses **left/right**, follows multi-step instructions, connects soccer to **counting, patterns, and directions** |

→ Building blocks: [index/05](../index/05-psychology-and-mental.md), [index/13](../index/13-teaching-cues-and-acronyms.md)

## Assessment checkpoints

Light-touch, encouraging, **development-focused** — never ranking.

- **Rubric:** a simple **Four-Corner / TIPS** scorecard per child → [index/04](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md).
- **Cadence:** a quick check at **start, mid-year, and before 9** — note growth on each child's **Individual Development Plan (IDP)**.
- **Signature checks for age 8:** juggling attempts, both-feet passing, "tries a move in a game," "bounces back from a mistake," "shares the ball."
- Celebrate **effort, bravery, and trying things** over outcomes.
