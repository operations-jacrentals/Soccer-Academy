## Where age 8 sits ✅

- **LTAD:** **FUNdamentals (~6–9)** on the cusp of **Learn to Train** (the "golden age"). → [Ford et al. 2011](../references/sources.md)
- **US Soccer / FA grassroots (Foundation Phase):** ball mastery, dribbling, 1v1, and **fun** are the priorities; US Soccer's U7/U8 theme is literally **"Me, the ball, and my friend."** → [sources](../references/sources.md)
- **Academy-model stage:** Ajax **Stage 2** — *technique & creativity* (⚠️ the stage-age split is secondary; see [research](../research/ajax-de-toekomst-model.md)).
- **One-line goal:** *fall in love with the ball, and become a brave, two-footed dribbler who tries things.* ✏️

## Format & equipment ✅ (US Soccer PDI 2017 · IFAB)

- A chronological **8-year-old spans US Soccer U8→U9**: **4v4, no goalkeeper, ball size 3** (U8) → **7v7 with GK, ball size 4** (U9). Confirm each child by **birth year**. → [sources](../references/sources.md)
- **Higher-standard note (Rule 2):** US Soccer *intentionally* keeps formats small at young ages for more touches, so the "higher standard" here is the **developmentally-optimal smaller format**, not a bigger one. We run **4v4-centric at 8**, introducing 7v7 as they move toward U9.

## How we teach it ✅ (method)

- **Play-Practice-Play (PPP)**, **one focus per session**, **one ball per pair**, games not lines. → [US Soccer](../references/sources.md)
- **Development over winning**; whole child across the **Four Corners**. → [FA](../references/sources.md)
- **Words of the year** ✏️ (our pick from [index/13](../index/13-teaching-cues-and-acronyms.md)): **"Head up" · "Both feet" · "Try it!" · "Find space" · "Next play."**

## Design around these characteristics ✅ (US Youth Soccer)

- Attention is **one task at a time**; high need for **adult approval/encouragement**; players **enjoy a partner** but struggle in big groups; **matches are a means to development, not the aim.** → [sources](../references/sources.md)

> **Reading the tables:** *Coming in* = a reasonable start-of-year floor for a
> typical 8-year-old with prior play; *Before 9* = our projected year-end target.
> Both columns are **✏️ coaching judgment aligned to the sourced priorities above**
> — for a **true beginner**, treat "Coming in" as the first-term target.

## 1 · Technical `[T]` ✏️

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Dribbling / ball mastery** | runs with the ball in space, dominant foot | changes direction inside/outside/sole; **both feet** more even; keeps it close in a crowd; head up at times |
| **First touch** | stops a rolling ball, inside foot | cushions a firmer pass; touch into space; either foot; begins to **"check shoulders"** before receiving |
| **Passing** | short inside-foot push pass to a near target | accurate short inside-foot pass with the right weight; **both feet**; first **give-and-go**; passes to a moving teammate |
| **Finishing** | kicks at goal (toe/laces mix) | **side-foot** placement up close; **laces** drive with a short run-up; either foot |
| **1v1 / creativity** | tries to run past a defender | 2–3 foundational moves (**body feint, step-over, drag-back, stop-and-go**) *attempted in games* |
| **Intro defending** | chases the ball | begins to **jockey** (stay on feet); pokes the ball away; simple **goal-side** idea |
| **Intro goalkeeping** | picks the ball up | tries GK: ready position, scoop a rolling ball, roll/throw it out |

→ blocks: [index/01](../index/01-technical-skills.md), [index/02](../index/02-skill-moves.md)

## 2 · Physical `[P]` ✏️ (aligned to LTAD FUNdamentals ✅)

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Movement skills (ABCs)** | runs, jumps, hops; changes direction awkwardly | **agility, balance, coordination** improved through games; smoother decelerate-and-turn |
| **Physical literacy** | short bursts of active play | sustains active play across a session — **through games, no formal fitness** (LTAD) |

→ blocks: [index/04](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md)

## 3 · Psychological `[Ps]` ✏️

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Enjoyment & confidence** | enjoys playing; can be shy | **brave to try things and make mistakes**; attempts 1v1s and moves |
| **Focus & effort** | short attention (one task at a time ✅) | holds focus for short activities; follows 1–2 step instructions; effort valued |
| **Coping with mistakes** | gets frustrated by errors | **"Next play"** — bounces back; mistakes are how we learn |

→ blocks: [index/05](../index/05-psychology-and-mental.md)

## 4 · Social / Character `[S]` ✏️

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Teamwork & sharing** | "beehive" — everyone chases the ball | begins to **pass/share** and **find space**; plays *with* teammates, takes turns |
| **Listening & respect** | needs reminders to listen | listens, waits a turn, **respects teammates, opponents, and rules** |
| **Responsibility** | needs help with kit/water | manages own **kit and water**; helps set up / pack up |

→ blocks: [index/05](../index/05-psychology-and-mental.md), [index/11](../index/11-player-development-and-pathways.md)

## 5 · Game understanding ✏️ (format ✅)

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Playing 4v4** (U8 ✅) | follows the ball in a clump | begins to **spread out / find space**; knows which goal is ours/theirs |
| **Rules & format** | knows "score in the goal" | knows **throw-in, in/out of play, no hands (except GK)**, restart basics; **4v4, ball size 3** (U8) → 7v7/size 4 (U9) ✅ |

→ blocks: [index/07](../index/07-tactics-and-systems.md), [index/12](../index/12-laws-and-formats.md)

## 6 · Smarts (soccer + smarts) ✏️

| Topic | Coming in | Before 9 |
|---|---|---|
| **Thinking / decisions** | reacts on instinct | simple **dribble-vs-pass** choices with prompting; **"head up / look"** forming |
| **Academic bridge** | counts, follows simple instructions | **counts touches/reps**, uses **left/right**, follows multi-step instructions; links soccer to counting, patterns, directions |

→ blocks: [index/05](../index/05-psychology-and-mental.md), [index/13](../index/13-teaching-cues-and-acronyms.md)

## Assessment checkpoints

- **Rubric:** a simple **Four-Corner** (✅ FA) / **TIPS** (✅ Ajax) scorecard per child → [index/04](../index/04-attributes-and-physical.md). The specific age-8 checks below are ✏️.
- **Cadence:** quick check at **start, mid-year, before 9**; note growth on each child's **Individual Development Plan (IDP)**.
- **Signature checks (✏️):** both-feet passing, "tries a move in a game," "bounces back from a mistake," "shares the ball," sustained focus on one task.
- Celebrate **effort, bravery, and trying** over outcomes (US Youth Soccer: high need for adult approval ✅).
