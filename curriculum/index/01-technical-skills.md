# 01 · Technical Skills

The core on-ball and defensive actions of the game — the "verbs" of soccer.
Grouped by theme; each is a strand that runs from foundational to elite.
Tags: difficulty `F/I/A/E`, four-corner `[T][P][Ps][S]`, LTAD stage, positions.

---

## Ball mastery & control

The foundation everything else is built on. Comfort with the ball on every
surface of both feet.

- **Surfaces of the foot** — inside, outside, instep (laces), sole, heel, toe. The vocabulary of every touch. `F` `[T]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **First touch / receiving** — cushioned touch to kill the ball; the single highest-leverage skill in the game. `F→E` `[T][Ps]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **Cushion vs. wedge control** — softening the ball vs. trapping against the ground. `F` `[T]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **Directional first touch** — receiving *into space* / away from pressure, not just stopping it. `I` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Receiving on the half-turn / open body** — opening up to play forward. `I→A` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Scanning / checking shoulders** — looking around *before* receiving to build a mental picture; "scan first, receive second." The perceptual habit behind good first touch and decisions. `F→E` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Aerial control** — thigh, chest, instep, head to bring a bouncing/dropping ball down. `I` `[T][P]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Close control** — keeping the ball within playing distance in tight areas. `F→A` `[T][P]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **Shielding / screening** — using the body to protect the ball from a defender. `F→A` `[T][P][Ps]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Juggling** — touch, rhythm, and coordination developer (both feet, thigh, head). `F→E` `[T][P]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **Turning** — inside/outside hook, drag-back, Cruyff (see [02](02-skill-moves.md)). `F→A` `[T]` `FUN` `ALL`

## Passing & distribution

- **Short/push pass (inside foot)** — the game's most-used action; accuracy + weight. `F` `[T]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **Driven pass** — firm, flat, laces, for range and speed. `I` `[T][P]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Lofted / long pass** — clearing distance and lines. `I→A` `[T][P]` `T2T` `ALL`
- **Through ball / penetrative pass** — breaking a line into space behind. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Disguised / no-look pass** — deceiving the defender's read. `A→E` `[T][Ps]` `T2C` `MID·FWD`
- **Outside-of-foot pass** — bent/quick release with the outside. `A` `[T]` `T2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Chipped / lofted through pass** — over a defender or keeper. `A` `[T]` `T2T` `MID·FWD`
- **One-touch / first-time passing** — speeding up the game; combination play. `I→E` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Wall pass (give-and-go / 1-2)** — the fundamental combination. `I` `[T][S]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Switch of play** — changing the point of attack to the free side. `I→A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `DEF·MID`
- **Backheel / flick** — close-quarters disguise. `A` `[T]` `T2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Weighting & timing** — the *quality* dimension across all passes. `F→E` `[T][Ps]` `FUN` `ALL`

- **Using both feet** — genuine two-footedness. `F→E` `[T]` `FUN` `ALL`
- **Feints & body deception** — selling a move before it happens. `I→E` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `MID·FWD`

## Shooting & finishing

- **Side-foot finish** — placement over power; the reliable finish. `F→A` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Instep drive** — power shot with the laces. `I` `[T][P]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Placement vs. power** — decision of *how* to finish. `I→A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `FWD`
- **First-time finish** — striking without controlling. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `FWD`
- **Volley & half-volley** — striking a dropping/bouncing ball. `A→E` `[T][P]` `T2C` `FWD`
- **Chip / dink** — lifting over an advancing keeper. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2C` `FWD`
- **Curled / bent finish** — around the keeper into the far corner. `A` `[T]` `T2T` `FWD`
- **1v1 vs. keeper** — composure and finishing choice under duress. `A→E` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `FWD`
- **Finishing from crosses** — timing runs, attacking near/far post, headers. `I→A` `[T][P][Ps]` `T2T` `FWD`
- **Poacher's finish** — reacting to rebounds/scraps in the box. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `FWD`
- **Long-range shooting** — striking from distance. `I→A` `[T][P]` `T2T` `MID`
- **Penalties** — technique + psychology (see [05](05-psychology-and-mental.md)). `I→E` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `ALL`
- **Free-kick striking** — bending, dipping, knuckleball. `A→E` `[T]` `T2C` `MID·FWD`

- **Driven cross** — low and hard across the face. `I→A` `[T][P]` `T2T` `DEF·MID·FWD`
- **Whipped cross** — bending away from the keeper. `A` `[T]` `T2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Cut-back** — pulled back to the edge of the box. `I→A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `MID·FWD`
- **Early cross** — delivered before the defense sets. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2C` `MID·FWD`

- **Interception** — reading and stepping into a passing lane. `I→A` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` `ALL`
- **Marking** — touch-tight vs. ball-side; goal-side positioning. `I→A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `DEF·MID`
- **Tracking runners** — following runs from midfield/deep. `I` `[T][Ps][P]` `T2T` `DEF·MID`
- **Recovery run** — sprinting back to reorganize after being beaten. `I` `[P][Ps]` `T2T` `ALL`
- **Pressing technique** — angle of approach, curved run, showing a side. `I→A` `[T][P][Ps]` `T2T` `ALL`
- **Blocking / shot-blocking** — getting body/legs in the shooting line. `I` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` `DEF`
- **Clearances** — decisive removal of danger (height, distance, width). `F→I` `[T][P]` `L2T` `DEF`

- **Set / ready position** — balanced, on toes, hands ready. `F` `[T][P]` `FUN` `GK`

- **Diving technique** — low, mid, high; collapse dives. `I→A` `[T][P]` `T2T` `GK`
