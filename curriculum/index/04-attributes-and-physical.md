# 04 · Attributes (Four Corners) & Physical

The qualities that make up a player. Organized by the **four-corner model**, then
a deeper catalog of physical attributes and how they're trained. Attributes are
what curriculum outcomes and assessments ultimately target.

---

## The four-corner model

The FA framework — develop the whole player, not just the footballer.

| Corner | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Technical / Tactical `[T]`** | ball skills + game understanding, decisions, positioning |
| **Physical `[P]`** | speed, strength, endurance, agility, coordination, movement |
| **Psychological `[Ps]`** | motivation, confidence, focus, resilience, competitiveness |
| **Social `[S]`** | communication, teamwork, character, discipline, respect |

Great sessions and great players are balanced across all four. This index tags
every item with its corners so the curriculum can stay balanced.

## Technical / tactical attributes `[T]`

First touch · passing (short & long) · dribbling · finishing · heading · tackling ·
crossing · technique · both feet · set-piece delivery · ball control · vision ·
decision-making · anticipation · positioning · off-the-ball movement · game
intelligence. *(Detailed under [01](01-technical-skills.md), [06](06-positions-and-roles.md), [07](07-tactics-and-systems.md).)*

## Physical attributes `[P]`

Speed & movement
- **Acceleration** — 0–10m explosiveness; often more decisive than top speed. `[P]`
- **Maximum speed / top-end velocity** — flat-out running. `[P]`
- **Agility / change of direction** — decelerate, reorient, re-accelerate. `[P]`
- **Quickness & reaction time** — first-step and response speed. `[P]`
- **Repeated-sprint ability (RSA)** — recovering to sprint again and again. `[P]`

Endurance & energy systems
- **Aerobic endurance** — sustaining effort over 90 minutes. `[P]`
- **Anaerobic capacity** — high-intensity bursts and tolerance. `[P]`
- **Stamina / work capacity** — total volume of work maintained. `[P]`

Strength & power
- **Maximal strength** — force production (age-appropriate for youth). `[P]`
- **Explosive power** — rate of force (jumps, sprints, duels). `[P]`
- **Jumping / leaping** — aerial ability for headers. `[P]`
- **Core & trunk strength** — stability for duels, shooting, turning. `[P]`

Coordination & control
- **Balance** — static and dynamic; holding off challenges. `[P]`
- **Coordination** — smooth, efficient multi-limb movement. `[P]`
- **Flexibility / mobility** — range of motion, injury resilience. `[P]`
- **Body composition & durability** — robustness, staying available. `[P]`

Fundamental movement skills (ABCs) — **A**gility, **B**alance, **C**oordination, plus running, jumping, landing. The athletic foundation laid in `FUN`/`L2T` before sport-specific loading.

## Physical training methods

- **SAQ (Speed, Agility, Quickness)** — ladders, hurdles, cones, sprint mechanics.
- **Sprint / speed training** — acceleration drills, flying sprints, resisted/assisted runs.
- **Plyometrics** — jumps, bounds, hops for reactive power (dosed carefully in youth).
- **Strength & resistance** — bodyweight → loaded, with a long runway before heavy lifting in adolescence; technique first.
- **Energy-system / conditioning** — intervals, fartlek, repeated-sprint sets, and *SSGs used as conditioning* (game-realistic fitness).
- **Mobility & flexibility** — dynamic warm-ups, movement flows, targeted range work.
- **Injury-prevention** — programs like FIFA 11+ (balance, strength, landing mechanics, hamstring/ACL focus).
- **Recovery** — sleep, nutrition, hydration, load spacing (the *other* half of adaptation).

## Growth, maturation & youth cautions

- **Peak Height Velocity (PHV)** — the growth-spurt window; coordination and injury risk shift around it (see [09](09-metrics-and-statistics.md), [11](11-player-development-and-pathways.md)).
- **Relative Age Effect (RAE)** — birth-month bias in selection; watch for it.
- **Bio-banding** — grouping by maturity, not just age, for fairer, better development.
- **Avoid early over-loading** — heavy strength/plyo and adult conditioning are inappropriate pre-adolescence; build movement quality and athletic literacy first.

> **Curriculum note:** In the young years, physical development = **movement
> skill and coordination** (ABCs), delivered through fun and games — *not* fitness
> drills. Sport-specific strength and conditioning ramp up through and after PHV.
