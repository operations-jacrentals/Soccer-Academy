# 02 · Skill Moves & 1v1 Feints

The dribbling moves, feints, and turns players use to beat an opponent, escape
pressure, or create separation. Grouped by difficulty tier. Every move has a
**purpose** (create space, change direction, disguise intent) — teach the *why*
and the *when*, not just the mechanics.

Tags: difficulty `F/I/A/E`, four-corner `[T][P][Ps]`, LTAD stage, best context.

> **Coaching principle:** A skill move is a *problem-solving tool*, not a trick.
> The best 1v1 players own 2–3 reliable moves off both feet and know exactly
> when to use them — not a highlight reel of twenty half-learned ones.

---

## Turns (escaping & changing direction)

- **Inside hook** — cut the ball back across the body with the inside of the foot. `F` `[T]` `FUN` · tight space
- **Outside hook** — turn with the outside of the foot. `F` `[T]` `FUN` · tight space
- **Drag-back** — sole of the foot pulls the ball back to reverse direction. `F` `[T]` `FUN` · receiving with back to goal
- **Cruyff turn** — fake a pass/shot, drag the ball behind the standing leg, turn away. `F→I` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` · wingers, escaping a marker
- **Stop-turn / V-pull** — stop the ball with the sole, pull it back in a V. `F` `[T]` `FUN` · changing angle
- **Half-turn / open-body receive** — pre-turn on receiving to face forward. `I` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` · midfield

## Foundational moves (`F`)

- **Body feint / body swerve** — drop a shoulder and shift weight to send the defender the wrong way. The root of all feints. `F` `[T][Ps]` `FUN` · anywhere 1v1
- **Step-over (single)** — circle the foot over the ball to fake a direction. `F→I` `[T][P]` `L2T` · wing
- **Stop-and-go / change of pace** — decelerate then explode. Beats defenders without any fancy touch. `F` `[T][P]` `FUN` · open space
- **Sole roll / ball roll** — roll the ball sideways across the body with the sole. `F` `[T]` `FUN` · tight space
- **Fake shot / fake pass** — wind up, then don't; freezes the defender. `F→I` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` · edge of box, wing

## Intermediate moves (`I`)

- **Scissors (double step-over)** — two step-overs to exaggerate the fake. `I` `[T][P]` `L2T` · wing
- **La Croqueta** — quick two-touch push of the ball from one foot to the other, across a lunging defender. `I` `[T][P]` `L2T` · central, tight
- **Chop (inside/outside)** — sharp cut across the body to change direction ~90°. `I` `[T]` `L2T` · wing, escaping pressure
- **Matthews feint** — push the ball slightly one way with the inside, then push it the other with the outside and accelerate. `I` `[T][P]` `L2T` · 1v1 wide
- **Drag-back + push** — drag back to bait, then push past. `I` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` · tight
- **Roulette / Marseille turn (Maradona/Zidane spin)** — 360° spin over the ball with both soles to shield and escape two defenders. `I→A` `[T][P][Ps]` `T2T` · congested midfield
- **Cut inside (inverted winger move)** — chop the ball from wide onto the stronger foot to shoot/cross. `I` `[T][Ps]` `L2T` · inverted winger

## Advanced moves (`A`)

- **Elastico / flip-flap** — push the ball out with the outside, snap it back with the inside in one motion. `A→E` `[T][P]` `T2C` · 1v1 wide
- **Reverse elastico** — inside-then-outside variant. `A→E` `[T][P]` `T2C` · wing
- **Ronaldo chop** — chop the ball behind the standing leg at speed to cut back inside. `A` `[T][P]` `T2T` · wing
- **Nutmeg (panna)** — push the ball through the defender's legs and collect. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` · tight 1v1
- **Berba spin / drag-spin** — drag-back combined with a spin away. `A` `[T][P]` `T2T` · back to goal
- **McGeady spin** — inside-of-both-feet spin to turn away from a defender on the touchline. `A` `[T][P]` `T2C` · wing
- **Hocus-pocus / around-the-world combos** — layered outside-inside feints. `A→E` `[T]` `T2C` · showboat/practice tool
- **Fake-and-cut (double move)** — chaining a feint into a cut to beat a recovering defender. `A` `[T][Ps]` `T2T` · wing

## Elite / flair (`E`)

- **Rainbow flick (okocha/rabona lift)** — flick the ball up and over a defender from behind. `E` `[T][P]` `T2C` · rare, open space
- **Rabona** — striking/crossing by wrapping the kicking leg behind the standing leg. `E` `[T]` `T2C` · wide delivery on weak side
- **Sombrero / over-the-top flick** — lift the ball over a pressing defender and run onto it. `E` `[T][Ps]` `T2C` · pressing escape
- **Around-the-world / juggling flourishes** — coordination/showcase, low match utility. `E` `[T]` `T2C` · practice, confidence

---

## Move selection by context (quick reference)

| Context | Go-to moves |
|---|---|
| Winger 1v1, touchline | step-over, scissors, Matthews, Ronaldo chop, cut-inside |
| Central, tight/congested | La Croqueta, roulette, nutmeg, drag-back |
| Back to goal | drag-back, Cruyff, Berba spin, shielding turn |
| Escaping a press | Cruyff, roulette, sombrero, quick 1-2 (see [01](01-technical-skills.md)) |
| Creating a shot | fake shot, cut inside, chop |

> **Curriculum note:** Introduce feints in the `L2T` "golden age" when
> coordination and confidence peak. Emphasize **both feet**, **game realism**
> (add a defender early), and **decision-making** — *which* move solves *this*
> defender — over move count.
