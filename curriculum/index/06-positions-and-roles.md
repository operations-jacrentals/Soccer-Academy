# 06 · Positions & Roles

Where players play and *how* they play there. A **position** is a zone on the
pitch; a **role** is a job within it; a **duty** is how aggressive that job is
(defend / support / attack). Modern football is role-rich — the same position
can be played many ways.

Tags: key attributes, in-possession (IP) and out-of-possession (OOP) duties.

---

## Goalkeeper (GK)

- **Shot-stopper** — classic keeper; positioning, reflexes, handling. *Key:* reactions, handling, positioning.
- **Sweeper-keeper** — defends space behind a high line, quick off the line. *Key:* aggression, decisions, rushing out, footwork.
- **Ball-playing keeper** — starts attacks with feet; comfortable under a press. *Key:* passing, composure, distribution.

## Full-backs & wing-backs (wide defenders)

- **Full-back (FB)** — defends the flank, supports attack. *Key:* stamina, 1v1 defending, crossing.
- **Attacking full-back** — overlaps, delivers crosses, high up the pitch.
- **Defensive / no-nonsense full-back** — stays home, prioritizes containment.
- **Wing-back (WB)** — the whole flank in a back-three system; huge running load. *Key:* engine, crossing, work rate.
- **Inverted full-back** — steps into midfield in possession to build/overload the center. *Key:* passing, positioning, tactical IQ.
- **Overlap / underlap** — running outside vs. inside the winger to stretch the defense.

## Centre-backs (CB)

- **Ball-playing defender (BPD)** — defends and starts play with progressive passing. *Key:* passing, composure, positioning.
- **Stopper** — aggressive, steps out to challenge, wins duels front-foot. *Key:* aggression, strength, heading.
- **Cover / sweeper CB** — reads danger, covers behind, mops up. *Key:* anticipation, pace, positioning.
- **No-nonsense CB** — clears danger simply, dominates the box. *Key:* heading, strength, bravery.
- **Wide centre-back** — outer CB in a back three; can step wide or carry forward. *Key:* pace, passing, discipline.

## Defensive midfield (the "6")

- **Anchor / holding midfielder** — screens the back line, sits, simple + safe. *Key:* positioning, tackling, discipline.
- **Deep-lying playmaker (regista)** — dictates tempo from deep, long-range passing. *Key:* vision, passing, composure.
- **Ball-winning midfielder (destroyer)** — hunts the ball, breaks up play. *Key:* work rate, tackling, stamina, aggression.
- **Half-back** — drops between/beside the CBs in build-up (aids a back-three build). *Key:* passing, positioning.

## Central midfield (the "8")

- **Box-to-box** — contributes in both boxes; all-round engine. *Key:* stamina, all-round skill, work rate.
- **Central playmaker / roaming playmaker** — orchestrates, finds space to receive and create. *Key:* vision, passing, movement.
- **Mezzala** — a "half-winger" #8 who drifts into the half-space to create and arrive late. *Key:* dribbling, movement, finishing.
- **Carrilero** — a shuttler covering the half-space between lines; balance and industry. *Key:* work rate, positioning.

## Attacking midfield (the "10")

- **Advanced playmaker / #10** — the creative hub between midfield and attack. *Key:* vision, technique, passing, flair.
- **Shadow striker / second striker** — arrives late into the box to score. *Key:* finishing, movement, off-the-ball.
- **Trequartista / enganche** — free creative role, low defensive duty, pure creation. *Key:* creativity, technique, passing.

## Wide forwards / wingers

- **Traditional winger** — hugs the touchline, beats the FB, crosses. *Key:* pace, dribbling, crossing.
- **Inside forward / inverted winger** — starts wide, cuts inside to shoot/create on the stronger foot. *Key:* finishing, dribbling, off-the-ball.
- **Wide playmaker** — drifts inside to create rather than beat-and-cross. *Key:* passing, vision, technique.
- **Wide target / raumdeuter ("space interpreter")** — reads and attacks pockets of space rather than dribbling. *Key:* movement, anticipation, finishing.

## Strikers / centre-forwards

- **Poacher / fox-in-the-box** — lives on the shoulder, finishes chances, minimal build-up. *Key:* finishing, anticipation, composure.
- **Target man** — holds the ball up, wins aerials, brings others in. *Key:* strength, heading, hold-up play.
- **Complete forward** — does it all: score, link, run, press. *Key:* all-round attacking quality.
- **False 9** — drops into midfield to overload and drag markers, vacating space. *Key:* passing, movement, technique, IQ.
- **Deep-lying forward** — links play from a withdrawn position. *Key:* passing, technique, movement.
- **Pressing forward** — leads the defensive effort from the front. *Key:* work rate, stamina, aggression.

## Roles vs. duties (a mental model)

- **Role** = the *style* of the job (e.g., ball-playing defender).
- **Duty** = the *aggressiveness* — **Defend**, **Support**, or **Attack** — how far and often the player pushes.
- A balanced team blends duties so it's never all-attack (exposed) or all-defend (toothless). This "rest structure" is the bridge to [07 · Tactics](07-tactics-and-systems.md).

## Universal responsibilities (every position)

In possession: create/keep width & depth, offer passing angles, make/attack space.
Out of possession: press or contain, mark, cover, track runners, hold shape.
Transition: react to the ball's change of hands *instantly* (counter or counter-press).

> **Curriculum note:** For young players, **rotate positions widely** — early
> specialization narrows development. Teach the *principles* every position shares
> before drilling role-specific detail; role identity firms up in the `T2T`+ years.
