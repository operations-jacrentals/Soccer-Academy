# 13 · Teaching Cues, Acronyms & Coaching Phrases

A deliberate library of **labels** — acronyms, mnemonics, identity slogans, and
short coaching cues — that make concepts **memorable and teachable** to players
and coaches. A good phrase does more than a paragraph: kids remember "scan first,
receive second" long after they'd forget a lecture on perception-action coupling.

> **Policy:** we *keep and grow* vocabulary. Even when we don't adopt a club's
> whole method, we keep its useful words. Adopt any of these, and coin our own.
> Each entry notes the **concept** it labels and **where it's detailed**.

## Coaching cues & phrases (say these on the pitch)

Short, sticky, repeatable. Say the cue, don't explain the theory.

- **"Train brains before bodies"** — technique + decisions over athleticism. → [03](03-training-methodology-and-drills.md), [merged model](../research/merged-model.md)
- **"Scan first, receive second" / "Check your shoulders"** — look before you get the ball. → [01](01-technical-skills.md)
- **"Play the way you're facing"** — simplest option unless you can turn safely. → [01](01-technical-skills.md)
- **"First touch out of your feet / into space"** — receive to your next action, not to a stop. → [01](01-technical-skills.md)
- **"Creators, not tacticians"** — protect creativity & 1v1s in the youngest years. → [11](11-player-development-and-pathways.md)
- **"The ball is the master"** — everything starts with the ball; maximize touches. → [03](03-training-methodology-and-drills.md)
- **"Development over winning"** — the result is a by-product. → [11](11-player-development-and-pathways.md)
- **"Next play"** — bounce back from a mistake, short memory. → [05](05-psychology-and-mental.md)
- **"Head up"** — perception, awareness, options.
- **"Show for it / angles and options"** — off-ball movement to support the ball. → [07](07-tactics-and-systems.md)
- **"Press the pass, not the player"** — press on the right cue/trigger. → [07](07-tactics-and-systems.md)
- **"Win it back in 5"** — counter-press immediately after losing it (gegenpressing, kid-sized). → [07](07-tactics-and-systems.md)

## Our own cues (to coin)

Reserve space for **Soccer-Academy's own vocabulary** — memorable labels we invent
for our drills, values, and standards. A shared, playful language is part of a
club's identity and a powerful teaching tool. Add them here as we create them.

> **Curriculum note:** For each age (8→13) pick a small, age-appropriate set of
> cues to make "the words of the year." Young players master a handful of phrases
> deeply rather than a glossary shallowly — introduce a few, use them relentlessly,
> then build on them next year.
